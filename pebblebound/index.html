1Introduction & Core Philosophy

Pebblebound ("we", "us", or "our"), published by Tech2U Savannah, is an offline-first arcade runner game built for one-thumb gameplay. We believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our privacy philosophy is founded upon radical data minimization: we collect, transmit, store, and sell zero personal data.

Pebblebound operates 100% offline. No user registration, no server analytics, no location tracking, and no external advertising networks are bundled in this release.

2Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

All data generated during your gameplay experience is stored strictly on your local device hardware using encrypted/sandboxed local device storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Gameplay Statistics: High scores for Trail, Zen Stones, and Daily Carve modes, total lifetime amber gathered, rows cleared, and clean run achievements.
  • Customization Unlocks: Unlocked cosmetic stone materials (Terracotta, River, Chalk, Moss, Obsidian, Speckle, Sunstone, Moonstone).
  • User Preferences: Audio volume, haptic feedback toggles, reduce motion accessibility mode, and colour-blind shape indicators.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the application or wiping app storage automatically and permanently deletes all stored data.

3Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

Pebblebound only utilizes the minimum essential system hardware features required for tactile gameplay:

VIBRATE / Haptic Feedback

Used solely to deliver subtle tactile lane shifts and collision feedback. Can be disabled anytime in in-game Settings.

Zero Network Permissions

The core game does not request sensitive Android permissions such as Camera, Contacts, Microphone, Location, or Storage access.

4Zero Advertising & Offline Architecture

Pebblebound is designed as a standalone offline experience. There are no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral profilers, or in-app purchase billing systems embedded in the game. You can play without an active internet connection at all times.

5Children's Privacy Compliance (COPPA)

Pebblebound is suitable for all ages and complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy frameworks. We do not collect personal information from children or adults alike.

6GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • We do not collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.
  • Users maintain absolute control over their local data through standard Android application management.
