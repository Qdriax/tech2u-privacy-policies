1 Overview & Core Principles

Lanternly: Bubble Pop Shooter ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a relaxing, fair bubble shooter game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.lanternly).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Lanternly requires NO user accounts, NO cloud logins, NO personal profiles, and NO external backend servers. All puzzle progress, postcards, coins, high scores, and settings remain stored strictly on your local device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, contacts, or phone number.

Technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage

Lanternly saves your gameplay progress locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android local storage):

  • Journey level progress (levels 1–300+), stars, coins, and boosters inventory.
  • Daily Lantern challenge history, best scores, and streaks.
  • Moonlight endless mode personal high scores and records.
  • Postcard album collection unlocked every 5 levels.
  • Player settings: sound volume, music toggle, vibration haptics, reduced motion accessibility, and optional 09:00 morning reminder preference.
  • Backup codes: Player progress can be exported and imported offline via text backup codes without any online account.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time in Android application settings by clearing app storage.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Lanternly is supported by compliant mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~2719391933). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Lanternly strictly adhere to Google Play Better Ads Standards and NEVER interrupt active gameplay:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on quiet screens (Home, Album, Settings); never shown on the gameplay board during active play.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only after a win behind an explicit "Ad break" card; never before win 5, at most once every 3 wins, and minimum 3 minutes apart. The next level always waits for your tap.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary player choices to either receive +5 extra shots on the out-of-shots card or double win coins. Rewarded ads are never forced.
  • App Open Ads: None. The game launches directly into the night market without launch ads.

5 Device Permissions

Lanternly requests only essential Android permissions required for gameplay, audio, and compliant ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to query UMP consent and load compliant ads when connected to the internet.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the ad engine to detect network connectivity and prevent unnecessary network calls when offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic clicks when aiming, launching lanterns, popping clusters, and lighting boosters.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly if you enable the optional 09:00 Daily Lantern reminder in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Lanternly contains no mature themes, violence, or chat mechanisms. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You maintain full control over your privacy rights at all times:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can open and reconfigure your advertising privacy choices at any moment directly from the in-game Settings menu via the Privacy Settings button, which launches the Google UMP consent form.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally, clearing application storage in Android Settings instantly deletes all records and saves permanently.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You may also opt out of interest-based ads via Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
