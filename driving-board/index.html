1 Overview

Driving Board is an independent practice app for learner drivers, developed by Tech2U Savannah. It is not affiliated with NTSA, DVSA, or any licensing authority.

Driving Board requires no user account and operates without any developer-owned backend server. Your learning progress and preferences stay on your device.

2 What the App Stores on Your Device

To save your learning state locally, the app stores:

  • Your practice topic scores, mock test history, and driving drill results.
  • Your app settings, including your selected country (driving side), daily reminder preference, and the end time of any ad-free reward period you earned.

This data is stored only on your device using Android Room and DataStore. Cloud backup is disabled (allowBackup="false"), and we do not receive, see, or store this data on our own servers.

3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Driving Board displays ads provided by Google AdMob to keep the app free to use. Ad requests are not treated as child-directed, and the maximum ad content rating is set to "T" (Teen).

The app shows banner ads, native ads (labelled "Ad") in lists, occasional interstitial or app open ads between sessions (never during a question, mock test, or driving drill), and optional rewarded video ads that grant 30 minutes without ads.

Google AdMob

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address (used for approximate location), app interaction events, and diagnostic data.

4 Consent & Privacy Choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Driving Board uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request your consent before initializing ads or requesting any ad content.

You can review or withdraw your consent at any time by opening Settings > Privacy options inside the app (shown wherever consent regulations apply), or via your device's Google Ads settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5 Notifications & Permissions

If you opt in, Driving Board can show one local daily practice reminder at 08:00, automatically skipped on days you have already practised. The reminder is scheduled entirely on your device using Android WorkManager; no push notification server is used and no personal data is transmitted.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by Google AdMob and Google UMP to load consent forms and advertisements.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Optional. Requested only if you opt in to the local 08:00 daily practice reminder, and used to reschedule the local reminder after device reboot.

6 Children's Privacy

Driving Board is intended for learner drivers aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7 Deleting Your Data

Open Settings > Reset progress in the app to delete all stored quiz, mock test, and drill results from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored app data. Data processed by Google for advertising is managed through your Google account's ad settings.

8 Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with an updated effective date.
