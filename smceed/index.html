1 Introduction & Application Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and protection of information by Tech2U Savannah (a division of Qdriax) in connection with the mobile application SMCEED: SMC Course & Strategy (Package ID: com.smceed.smceed), hereinafter referred to as the "Application", "App", or "Service".

SMCEED is designed as an offline-first Smart Money Concepts (SMC) educational platform comprising 8 comprehensive trading modules, 64 interactive scenario-based quiz questions, high-resolution annotated chart figures, and structured micro-learning curricula spanning Market Structure, Order Flow, Inducement, Supply & Demand, Fair Value Gaps, Liquidity Sweeps, and Trade Management.

2 Financial Non-Intermediation & Trading Risk Disclaimer

STRICT EDUCATIONAL SCOPE (CFTC RULE 4.41 DISCLAIMER): SMCEED is strictly an educational and simulated training application. It does NOT provide financial advisory services, personalized investment recommendations, trading signals, automated execution, or portfolio management. Trading foreign exchange, futures, stocks, or commodities involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. Hypothetical or simulated performance results have certain inherent limitations. Past performance of any trading methodology is not indicative of future results.

Any mention of regulated brokers (e.g. HFM) inside the educational resources or app directory is provided purely for contextual reference and informational convenience. The Application does not handle deposits, execute trades, or access broker account credentials.

3 Data Minimization: What We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with global privacy frameworks (EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, California CCPA/CPRA), we practice strict data minimization. The Application does NOT collect, harvest, or transmit:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, home address, email address, or phone number.
  • Financial credentials, credit/debit card numbers, bank account details, or trading account passwords.
  • Precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • Device contact lists, call logs, SMS messages, camera, microphone, or biometric data.

No user account creation, registration, or cloud sign-in is required to access the full course content.

4 Local Offline Data Storage & Progress

All curriculum lessons, technical charts, quiz questions, and answer explanations are bundled directly into the local application package. Your study progress, including:

  • Completed module IDs and unlock statuses,
  • Quiz scores and percentage ratings,
  • Answer reviews and flagged questions, and
  • Interface theme preferences (Dark, Light, or System Default),

are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted Android SharedPreferences storage. This data is entirely offline, never transmitted to external cloud servers, and can be cleared instantly via the in-app "Reset All Progress" button or device application settings.

5 Device Permissions & Technical Purpose

SMCEED requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary for its intended features:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to query AdMob monetization servers, download advertising creatives, and enable outbound links to regulated educational references and app store profiles.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to determine whether an active network connection exists before attempting network calls.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used solely on Android 13+ (API 33+) to schedule an optional, local daily morning study reminder (08:00 AM) encouraging users to review Smart Money Concepts. No remote push notifications or tracking tokens are utilized.

6 Monetization, AdMob & Google UMP Consent Framework

To provide high-quality Smart Money Concepts education 100% free without charging subscriptions or paywalls, SMCEED is monetized through Google AdMob banner and interstitial advertisements.

Consent Management Compliance: We have integrated the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. In jurisdictions covered by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users are presented with a consent dialog prior to ad initialization. No personalized ads are requested without user consent.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can withdraw or adjust your consent choices at any time directly within the app by opening the Profile & More tab and selecting Privacy & Consent Preferences, which instantly invokes the Google UMP Privacy Options Form.

8 Notification System (Daily Study Reminders)

SMCEED includes a local background notification trigger designed to deliver a single daily morning educational prompt (e.g. "Master Smart Money Concepts: Continue your daily trading lesson"). These notifications are generated entirely on-device using the operating system's local alarm scheduler (flutter_local_notifications). No user identification, token collection, or remote tracking servers are involved. You can disable notifications at any time via Android App Settings.

9 Your Rights: Access, Correction & Consent Revocation

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess statutory rights regarding personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: All progress data resides locally on your device in plain readable format.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may erase all locally stored data instantly using the in-app "Reset All Progress" feature or by clearing App Data in your device settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of personalized interest-based advertising via the in-app UMP Privacy Preferences or device-level Google Ads settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID).

10 Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)

SMCEED is an advanced financial educational platform intended for adult users, professional students, and self-directed market analysts. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you become aware that a minor has provided data through third-party ad interactions, please contact us immediately to purge associated records.

11 Data Retention & Security Architecture

Because SMCEED does not maintain remote user databases or backend servers, we do not store, retain, or process user credentials. All local application files are secured within the Android sandbox environment protected by operating system-level encryption.
