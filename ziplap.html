1 Overview & Core Principles

Ziplap: Slot Car Racing ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a tabletop 3D slot-car racing game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.ziplap).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Ziplap requires NO accounts, NO cloud sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO backend databases. All race times, car unlocks, high scores, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, contacts, or phone number.

Any technical data processing is strictly performed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage

Ziplap saves your gameplay progress locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Race lap records and best times across 12 circuit layouts.
  • Daily Circuit calendar streaks and daily challenge scores.
  • Unlocked toy slot cars (GT, open-wheeler, bubble cars, and custom liveries).
  • Grand Prix career stats: total laps, win runs, tight corner streaks, and deslot recovery tallies.
  • Player settings: sound volume, background music toggle, haptic feedback, colour-blind geometric markers, reduced motion accessibility, low graphics profile, and daily 09:00 morning reminder preference.

This data never leaves your device and can be reset at any time in the in-game Settings menu or by clearing application data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Ziplap is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~8893083897). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Ziplap strictly adhere to Google Play Better Ads Standards and NEVER interrupt active races:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on menus, garage, and post-race result screens; automatically hidden during active racing.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only after a race behind an explicit "Ad break" notice; never during the first 3 races, at most once every 3 races, and minimum 2 minutes apart.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary player choices to either unlock a bonus car in the garage or arm a Marshal pass for the next race. Choosing a rewarded ad resets the interstitial frequency timer.
  • App Open Ads: Displayed only when returning to an idle home screen after at least 4 hours of inactivity.

5 Device Permissions

Ziplap requests only essential Android permissions required for gameplay, audio, and compliant ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to query UMP consent and load compliant ads when connected to the internet.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the ad engine to detect network connectivity and prevent unnecessary network calls when offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic clicks when switching lanes, cornering on the grip threshold, and deslotting.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly if you enable the optional 09:00 Daily Circuit reminder in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Ziplap contains no adult content, no violence, and no social chat mechanisms. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You maintain full control over your privacy rights at all times:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can open and reconfigure your advertising privacy choices at any moment directly from the in-game Settings menu via the Privacy Options button, which launches the Google UMP consent form.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally, clearing application storage in Android Settings instantly deletes all records and saves permanently.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You may also opt out of interest-based ads via Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
