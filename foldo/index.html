1 Overview & Core Principles

FOLDO ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is an arcade 3D paper-folding racing game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.foldo).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: FOLDO requires NO accounts, NO sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO cloud registration. All race progress, unlocked paper skins, level completion, high scores, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, sell, share, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, biometric data, contacts, or phone number.

Any technical data processing is strictly performed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as outlined below.

3 On-Device Storage

FOLDO saves your gameplay state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Grand Tour level progression and win streaks against paper rivals Inky and Scrap.
  • High scores, longest Crisp fold chains, and personal best records across Grand Tour, Daily Route, Snip Chase, and Relay game modes.
  • Unlocked paper skins (Kraft, Newsprint, Graph, Washi, Road Map, Polka, and Foil).
  • Accessibility and user preferences: sound effects, haptic vibrations, colour-blind mode with geometric markers, reduced motion, low quality graphics setting, and optional morning reminder preference.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

FOLDO is supported by player-respectful mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~2902780258). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in FOLDO strictly follow Google's Better Ads Standards and never interrupt active racing:

  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choices: (a) "Rerace" head start after a lost Grand Tour race, or (b) "Tape" second chance when snipped in Snip Chase (once per run).
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only when leaving a race result screen (never on new personal bests, with mandatory cooldown periods, and never during a race).
  • Adaptive Banner: Displayed under menus and results only. Banners are strictly disabled during active gameplay (Play, Paused, Offer, and Handover states).
  • App Open Ads: Displayed on cold start only, skipped on the first two launches, and strictly capped at most once every 4 hours.

5 Local Morning Reminder

FOLDO includes an optional daily morning reminder at 09:00 local time. This reminder is scheduled purely on your device using flutter_local_notifications. No push notifications servers, device tokens, or remote messaging systems are used. You can turn this reminder on or off at any time directly in the in-game Settings menu.

6 Device Permissions

FOLDO requests only the minimal technical permissions required for local operation and AdMob ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used strictly to communicate with Google AdMob servers for ad loading and consent verification. Races can be played fully offline.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables AdMob and UMP to check network connectivity before requesting ad assets.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Allows tactile haptic feedback when folding paper forms and hitting speed boosts. Can be disabled in Settings.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Requested only if you choose to enable the optional daily 09:00 morning reminder in Settings.

7 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

FOLDO is designed for a general audience and family-friendly casual play (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks, please contact us immediately to facilitate prompt removal.

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you maintain rights regarding data protection:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time by tapping the "Privacy Settings" button inside the FOLDO Settings screen. This immediately reopens the Google UMP consent preferences dialog.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally, clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all saved progress, skins, and local records.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
