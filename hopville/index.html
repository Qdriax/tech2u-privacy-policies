1 Overview & Core Principles

Hopville: Dice Board Game ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a fair, 3D dice board and cozy town-building game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.hopville).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Hopville requires NO user accounts, NO cloud logins, NO personal profiles, and NO external game backend servers. All game progress, dice stocks, town landmarks, postcards, and player settings remain strictly stored on your local device. Nobody can raid, steal from, or alter your town.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, contacts, or phone number.

Technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage

Hopville saves your gameplay progress locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android local preferences):

  • Town Tour building progress across all 8 towns (Pebble Harbour, Spice Bazaar, Savannah Junction, Maple Hollow, Frostpeak, Cactus Gulch, Coral Cove, Neon Heights) and 5 landmarks per town (1 to 3 stars).
  • Dice stock, coin bank, and regenerating dice timestamps.
  • Postcards collection (72 collectible postcards across 8 sets) with complete duplication protection.
  • Daily Board challenge completion records and personal streaks.
  • Player settings: sound volume, music toggle, vibration haptics, hints, and optional 09:00 morning reminder preference.
  • Offline backup codes: Progress can be exported and imported directly via plain text backup codes without any online account.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time in Android application settings by clearing app storage.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Hopville is supported by compliant mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~5775221108). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Hopville strictly adhere to Google Play Better Ads Standards and NEVER interrupt active dice rolls or gameplay:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed only on menus, score screens, and quiet sheets; never overlaid on the active 3D board.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only after a finished Daily Board, finished Party match, or completed town behind an explicit "Ad break" card; never during the first 3 minutes, at least 4 minutes apart, and at least 45 seconds after any rewarded ad.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary player choices to either collect +25 extra dice when low on rolls or double a Crate Grab or prize wheel win. Rewarded ads are never forced.
  • App Open Ads: None. Hopville opens cleanly directly onto the board with zero launch prestitials.

5 Device Permissions

Hopville requests only essential Android permissions required for gameplay, audio, and compliant ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to query UMP consent and load compliant ads when connected to the internet.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the ad engine to detect network connectivity and prevent unnecessary network calls when offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic pulses when rolling dice, tapping tiles, and collecting rewards.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly if you enable the optional 09:00 Daily Board reminder in Settings (Android 13+ asks your explicit permission first).

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Restores your scheduled 09:00 local reminder after device restart if you opted in.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Hopville contains no mature themes, violence, or chat mechanisms. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You maintain full control over your privacy rights at all times:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can open and reconfigure your advertising privacy choices at any moment directly from the in-game Settings menu via the Privacy Settings button, which launches the Google UMP consent form.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally, clearing application storage in Android Settings instantly deletes all records and saves permanently.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You may also opt out of interest-based ads via Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
