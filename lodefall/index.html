1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to Lodefall: Magnetic Descent (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed by Tech2U Savannah. We believe arcade games should be fast, responsive, and fully respectful of your privacy. Lodefall is built as an offline-first, client-rendered arcade descent game requiring no account creation, no sign-ins, and no personal profile harvesting.

Core Guarantee: All deterministic shaft generation, magnetic physics, collision checks, high scores, and audio synthesis execute 100% locally on your device hardware. We do not operate external gameplay servers or harvest personal user identities.

2 Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

Lodefall stores minimal gameplay preferences and progress solely on your device via Android sandboxed SharedPreferences:

  • Depth Records & Stats: Personal best depth (m), gold nuggets collected, unlocked cosmetic stones, and completed training status.
  • Daily Dive History: Best scores achieved for daily shared seed runs.
  • Preferences: Audio volume, haptic vibration toggle, and tutorial completion flags.

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage via Android Settings, this local data is permanently cleared.

3 Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

Lodefall requests only the bare minimum permissions required for operation:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used strictly to query user privacy consent via Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and display advertisements via Google AdMob when connected to a network.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to verify network connectivity before initiating ad requests, preventing unnecessary battery drain and network errors while playing offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used exclusively for tactile haptic feedback during near-misses, magnetic pulses, and obstacle collisions. Can be toggled on/off in Settings at any time.

4 Third-Party Advertising & Google UMP Consent

Lodefall is monetized via Google AdMob advertisements (interstitial ads between dives and optional rewarded video to revive a Free Dive once). We strictly comply with global privacy frameworks by integrating the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

Google AdMob & UMP SDK

Depending on your geographic location and consent selections, Google AdMob may process pseudonymous advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and device telemetry to deliver contextual or personalized ads.

5 Consent Revocation & User Privacy Choices

We respect your right to modify or withdraw consent at any time. When in an applicable jurisdiction (such as EEA, UK, Switzerland, or US states), you can access the Privacy & Ad Choices option directly inside the in-game Settings menu. Tapping this button immediately invokes Google UMP to reconfigure or revoke your ad choices.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Lodefall is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not feature user chat, public profiles, or location tracking.

7 Global Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Whether you reside in the European Union, the United Kingdom, California, or elsewhere globally, we honor your privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Erasure: Because gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your device, deleting the app or clearing app storage immediately and permanently removes all your data.
  • Opt-Out of Data Sale / Sharing: You can opt out of personalized ad targeting via the in-app Privacy & Ad Choices menu or your Android device's Google Ads settings.
