1 Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in the Math Quiz mobile application for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.mathquiz).

Core Privacy Principle: Math Quiz is designed to operate locally on your device without requiring user account creation, logins, or server-side telemetry tracking.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Math Quiz does not collect, transmit, or sell:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (such as names, phone numbers, or physical addresses).
  • Financial or payment credentials.
  • Location data or GPS telemetry.
  • Microphone, camera, or contact data.

3 Advertising & Google UMP Consent

Math Quiz displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-personalized or personalized advertising identifiers in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and US states (CCPA/CPRA), we deploy the certified Google UMP SDK to obtain user consent before serving ads. You can modify or withdraw your consent preferences at any time in the app settings.

4 Device Permissions

The app only requests standard operational Android permissions:

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required solely for fetching AdMob advertisements and checking network connectivity.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & VIBRATE

Required to reschedule daily practice reminders locally after a device reboot and deliver haptic feedback on answers.

5 Data Storage & Retention

All quiz progress, high scores, coins, and reminder preferences are stored exclusively in your device's local SQLite database and SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data completely.

6 Children's Privacy

Math Quiz complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
