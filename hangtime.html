1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") manages and protects your information in connection with the mobile game Hangtime: Ramp Car Stunt Jump (package name: com.hangtime.game).

Core Privacy Guarantee: Hangtime is engineered to be 100% playable offline with zero mandatory user registration. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or cloud profiles. Your high scores, jump stats, unlocked toy vehicles, and custom settings stay securely on your physical device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, contacts, or financial details.

Any technical network transmissions are strictly handled by certified mobile advertising partners (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) to provide compliant ad delivery, fraud prevention, and user privacy consent management as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage

Hangtime persists your gameplay progress locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the Capacitor WebView under hangtime.save):

  • High scores across game modes: Rally, Daily Route, Trick Shot, and Duel.
  • Lifetime stunt statistics: total runs, buses cleared, flips landed, perfect landings, and longest landing streaks.
  • Unlocked toy vehicles (buggy, van, pickup, hatch, monster truck, roadster, muscle car).
  • Player preferences: sound volume, procedural music, haptics, color-blind mode, reduced motion, graphics fidelity, and morning reminder toggle.

This data never leaves your device. You can erase all data at any time by clearing application data in your Android system settings or via the in-game Reset option.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Hangtime is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertisements served via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~1595223975). We integrate Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with global privacy standards, including:

  • European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR)
  • United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
  • Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA)

Consent First: For users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and relevant jurisdictions, personalized advertising identifiers and non-essential cookies are only activated after you provide consent through Google's interactive UMP consent dialog.

Ad implementations strictly follow Google Play Better Ads policies (ads never interrupt mid-run throttle or air jumps):

  • Rewarded Video: 100% voluntary. You may choose to watch an ad to revive your run after a crash and continue from the jump you missed.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped full-screen ads shown exclusively between finished runs (never during active gameplay, never on the first two runs, and separated by cooldown timers).
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed only on menu screens (Home, Garage, Stats, Settings). Banners are completely hidden during active stunt runs.

5 Device Permissions

Hangtime requests only the minimal technical permissions required for normal operation:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to fetch ad content and communicate with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) servers for consent management.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the ad SDK to check for network availability before initiating requests.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides crisp haptic feedback on car revs, takeoff, landings, and crashes. Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used solely if you explicitly enable the optional Morning Reminder (9:00 AM local notification) for the Daily Route jump challenge. Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) are explicitly disabled.

6 Children’s Privacy

Hangtime is designed as a general-audience arcade game (13+). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you discover that a minor has provided information without parental consent, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and international privacy laws, you have full authority over your data:

  • Review & Revoke Ad Consent: You can open the Google UMP privacy options form at any time inside the game via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices to update or withdraw consent.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, clearing the app's cache and data in your Android device settings permanently deletes all saved progress.
