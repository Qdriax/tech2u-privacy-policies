1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Bumpko: Bumper Car Sumo Battle (package name: com.bumpko.game).

Core Guarantee: Bumpko is built with a strict privacy-first foundation. We do not require accounts, logins, social profiles, email addresses, or cloud registration. All gameplay progression, high scores, unlocked clay bumper cars, and scrapbook records remain strictly stored on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Any technical data transmission is strictly limited to certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) necessary to serve compliant advertisements and manage user consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Bumpko saves game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • High scores across Gauntlet, Daily Tart, Rush Minute, and Couch Duel modes.
  • Scrapbook statistics: total runs, cars bumped in, best combo multipliers, furthest round reached, tarts cleared, close call edge escapes, and total time on the tart.
  • Unlocked clay bumper cars (Classic Bumper, Duckling, Teacup, Tugboat, Toaster, Snail, Pumpkin, Bathtub).
  • Audio and accessibility preferences: sound effects, haptic vibrations, colour-blind mode, motion reduction, low quality graphics setting, and daily morning reminder state.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing application storage in your Android settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Bumpko is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized advertising identifiers or ad requests are initialized until you have reviewed and submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Bumpko strictly adhere to Google's Better Ads Experiences rules (ads never appear while cars are on the tart):

  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choice to Continue once per Gauntlet run after falling off the tart.
  • Rewarded Interstitial: Completely optional player choice in the Garage to unlock a featured bumper car immediately.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only when leaving a run result screen behind a clear "Ad break" indicator (never in the first 2 runs, at most every 2nd run, at least 2 minutes apart, and reset whenever a rewarded ad is chosen).
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively on quiet screens (Home, menus, and result screens). Never displayed during active tart matches.

5 Device Permissions

Bumpko requests only the minimal technical permissions required for game features:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad inventory and load privacy consent forms via Google AdMob and Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to detect active network connectivity before sending requests.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Enables crisp tactile haptic feedback when bumping rival cars, dashing, or landing impacts (can be toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you explicitly opt-in to the 09:00 AM Morning Reminder for new Daily Tart puzzles. Permission is requested only after your second finished run and can be turned on or off anytime in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy

Bumpko does not knowingly target, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure appropriate protections or data purging.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection regulations, you retain full control over your privacy:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your consent choices at any time directly inside the game by opening Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing application storage in your Android settings.
