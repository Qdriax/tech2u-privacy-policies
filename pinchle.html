1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Pinchle: Dice & Sandcastles (package name: com.tech2u.pinchle).

Privacy-First Commitment: Pinchle requires no player accounts, no social sign-ins, no passwords, and no cloud registrations. All dice history, sandcastle tiers, shell balances, album collections, and crab cosmetic unlocks stay stored entirely on your local device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or biometric data.

Data transmission from the app is strictly restricted to certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) necessary to display compliant advertisements and manage player privacy choices in supported regions.

3 On-Device Storage

Pinchle saves gameplay state exclusively on your personal device using encrypted sandboxed storage (WebView localStorage and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Castle construction progression across 8 sunny beaches (Bucket Bay to Ember Shore).
  • Shell balance, roll tokens, and Lucky Tide pity meter charge.
  • Album collection (12 collected shells) and 5 unlocked crab hats.
  • Fair Dice audit records (exact count of every face rolled across all games).
  • Local game settings: sound effects, music, haptic vibrations, auto-hold preference, and morning reminder toggle.
  • Device transfer codes: player state can be securely transferred between devices via an offline code generated on-demand without any server storage.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Pinchle is 100% free to play with no in-app purchases, supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~2820317916). We integrate Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ensure compliance with global privacy regulations including EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, and US State privacy laws (such as CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: For players in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and relevant US jurisdictions, no personalized advertising identifiers or ad requests are processed until you have reviewed and submitted your consent preferences via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Pinchle strictly honors respectful, non-intrusive ad placements:

  • Rewarded Video: 100% optional player choice to earn bonus rolls or double treasure excavation rewards. Strictly one ad per reward.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only after completing a side game (Daily Tide, Duel, Shell Snatch) behind a transparent "Ad break" notice. Never shown during beach turns or gameplay.
  • Banners: Displayed exclusively on quiet screens (menus and score sheets). Never during active dice rolling.
  • Zero Pop-Ups: No pop-ups on launch, no pop-ups when running out of rolls.

5 Device Permissions

Pinchle requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary for gameplay features:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad inventory and present privacy consent dialogs via Google AdMob and Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to confirm network connectivity before attempting ad requests.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides crisp physical haptic feedback when rolling dice, flinging crabs, or digging treasure (can be toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used exclusively for the optional 09:00 AM Morning Reminder to alert you that daily rolls have refilled. Requires explicit player opt-in and can be disabled anytime in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy

Pinchle does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure appropriate protections.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection laws, you maintain complete sovereignty over your privacy:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, alter, or revoke your consent preferences at any time directly inside the game by tapping More → Privacy choices.
  • Right to Deletion: Because all game data is kept locally on your phone, you can permanently delete all game files at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings.
