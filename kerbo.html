1 Overview & Core Principles

Kerbo: Street Drift Racing ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a 3D street drift racing game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.kerbo).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Kerbo requires NO accounts, NO cloud sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO external player databases. All race career stars, car unlocks, garage tuning specs, drift records, and preferences remain stored strictly on your local device with double checksummed backup save slots and an offline transfer code.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, contact lists, or phone numbers.

Technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display privacy-compliant advertisements and maintain regional consent preferences as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage

Kerbo stores gameplay state entirely on your device using sandboxed storage (HTML5 localStorage in the embedded rendering environment and native Android SharedPreferences):

  • Career progression across 4 districts (Harbour Dusk, Neon Grid, Palm Strip, Skyline) and 20 events.
  • Garage inventory: 6 street cars, upgrade tuning stages (engine, tyres, nitro), and custom body paint choices.
  • Drift trial high scores, circuit records, and Daily Circuit completions.
  • Player configuration: music volume, sound effects toggle, vibration/haptics, far camera mode, left-handed controls, and morning reminder preference.
  • Two checksummed local backup slots and an exportable transfer code for moving progress to a new phone without any online accounts.

This data never leaves your device and can be permanently removed at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Kerbo is supported by fair, non-disruptive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~1346514796). We integrate Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy standards, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Kerbo strictly respect your gameplay and adhere to Google Play Better Ads Standards:

  • No In-Race Ads: Ads NEVER appear during active racing, starting countdowns, or pause screens.
  • No App-Open Ads: The app opens directly to the game without disruptive launch banners.
  • Menu Banners: Non-intrusive adaptive banners displayed only on menus (Home, Career, Garage, Daily Goals, Results).
  • Frequency-Capped Interstitials: Shown only between races, capped at a maximum of 1 per 3 races and 4 minutes, never during your first 4 races, and always preceded by a Ready countdown card.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary player choices to double cash winnings or test-drive a locked car. Choosing a rewarded ad resets the interstitial frequency timer.
  • Automatic Sound Sync: Disabling game sounds in Settings automatically mutes AdMob advertisements (setAppMuted(true)).

5 Device Permissions

Kerbo requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary for operation and compliant ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Allows the game to query UMP consent and load compliant ads when connected online.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the network monitor to avoid unnecessary ad requests when the device is offline.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Required on Android 13+ only if you choose to activate the optional 09:00 Daily Circuit reminder in Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows the local notification alarm to reschedule after a device reboot if you have active reminders enabled.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Kerbo contains stylized arcade street racing with no realistic violence, no blood, no adult content, and no open chat features. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), compliant with COPPA and GDPR Article 8.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You maintain full control over your privacy rights at all times:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review or withdraw your advertising consent choices at any moment directly inside the game settings via the Privacy Options / Consent preferences button powered by Google UMP.
  • Instant Data Deletion: Since all player progress is stored strictly on your device, clearing application data in Android Settings instantly and irreversibly wipes all local records.
  • Opt-Out of Interest-Based Advertising: You can reset or delete your advertising ID anytime via your Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
