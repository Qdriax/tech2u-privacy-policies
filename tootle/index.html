1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile arcade game Tootle: Train Track Switch (package name: com.tech2u.tootle).

Core Guarantee: Tootle is built with privacy-by-design. We do not require accounts, logins, cloud sync, email addresses, or social profiles. All gameplay progress, high scores, unlocked engines, shift statistics, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your local device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or contacts.

Any technical data processed is handled exclusively through Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to facilitate compliant advertising and consent choices, as detailed in Section 4.

3 On-Device Storage

Tootle stores your progress locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • High scores and performance records across Rush Hour, Daily Timetable, Express, and Two Drivers modes.
  • Logbook statistics: total trains delivered, signal lamp saves, shifts completed, and multiplier records.
  • Unlocked engines: Diesel Shunter, Old Timer, Tram, Bullet, Puffer, Mail Van, and Rocket.
  • Player settings: audio levels, tactile haptic toggles, colour-blind shape indicators, reduced motion mode, low quality render governor, and morning reminder preferences.

This data never leaves your device and can be wiped anytime by clearing the app data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Tootle is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~2245960665). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ensure full compliance with global privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent Management: For users in regulated jurisdictions (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no ad requests or personalized identifiers are initialized until consent preferences have been established via the interactive Google UMP dialog.

Ad formats and placement rules in Tootle comply strictly with Google Better Ads standards:

  • Rewarded Ads: Completely optional player choice to "Relight a lamp" (Rush Hour) or "+15 seconds" (Express), limited to once per run, or to test-drive a locked engine for one shift.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only when exiting a results screen behind a clear "Ad break" indicator (never before run 3, at most every 2nd run, at least 2 minutes apart).
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively on static screens (Home, menus, results). Never shown during active railway track routing.
  • App Open Ads: Gated strictly to returning after 4+ hours to an idle Home screen; never on cold start and never on the first two launches.

5 Device Permissions

Tootle requests only essential permissions necessary to operate:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad content and Google UMP consent forms.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the app to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting ad inventory.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides crisp haptic feedback when flipping brass track switches (can be disabled in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you explicitly enable the optional 09:00 AM morning reminder for the new Daily Timetable challenge. Asked once after your second shift and can be toggled on/off at any time in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Tootle is an arcade railway game designed for general audiences (ages 13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such information.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under global data privacy laws, you have full authority over your data:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game by opening Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Data Deletion: Since all player statistics are stored on-device, deleting the application or clearing application cache in Android Settings permanently removes all records.
