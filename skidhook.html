1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Skidhook: Rope Drift Racer (package name: com.tech2u.skidhook).

Core Guarantee: Skidhook is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not require account creation, logins, or cloud registration. All gameplay progression, high scores, unlocked vehicles, and local preferences remain strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Any data transmission is limited strictly to certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) necessary to serve compliant advertisements and manage user consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Skidhook saves game state locally using sandboxed device storage:

  • High scores, corners cleared, perfect releases, and milestone achievements.
  • Unlocked cars and visual garage selections.
  • Settings (sound effects, haptics, colorblind mode, motion reduction, morning reminder preferences).

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by resetting your logbook in Settings or clearing the app’s data via Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Skidhook is supported by advertising via Google AdMob. We implement Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable regions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized advertising identifiers or ad requests are initialized until you have reviewed and submitted your consent choices via the interactive consent form.

5 Device Permissions

Skidhook requests only the bare minimum technical permissions required for stable operation:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad inventory and load privacy consent forms via Google AdMob/UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to detect active internet connectivity.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Enables subtle tactile haptic feedback during hook latching, cornering, and drifting releases (can be toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used solely if you explicitly opt-in to the 09:00 AM Daily Road notification reminder. Can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy

Skidhook does not knowingly target, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure appropriate privacy protections are enforced.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection laws, you retain full control over your privacy:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your consent choices at any time directly inside the game by navigating to Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing application data in Android settings.
