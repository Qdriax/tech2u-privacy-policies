1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to Stillpoint: One-Tap Timing (“Stillpoint”, “we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed by Tech2U Savannah. We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and player privacy. Stillpoint is designed as an offline-first, client-rendered precision timing arcade game requiring no user registration, no account creation, and no collection of personal identity information.

Core Guarantee: All procedural round generation, timing detection down to the millisecond, 3D turntable graphics rendering, haptic triggers, score calculation, and unlocked cosmetic skins run 100% locally on your device. We do not operate player accounts or transmit personal identifying information to our servers.

2 Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

Stillpoint stores minimal gameplay preferences, statistics, and unlocked items locally on your device via sandboxed local storage:

  • Game Records & Statistics: High score, highest round reached, count of perfect freeze stops, timing accuracy percentage, and longest lock streak.
  • Unlocked Customization: Unlocked turntable cosmetic themes (Bakelite, Candy, Mint, Brass, Cocoa, Sherbet, Lacquer, and Clay).
  • Player Preferences: Sound effects switch, haptic vibration toggle, colour-blind palette mode, reduced motion mode, low graphics toggle, and daily morning reminder notification preference.
  • Daily Challenge History: Daily run scores and attempt completion flags.

This data remains completely sandboxed on your device and is never uploaded to Tech2U Savannah servers. If you clear the App's storage or uninstall Stillpoint, this data is permanently erased.

3 Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

Stillpoint requests only the explicit system permissions needed to deliver game functionality and compliant advertising:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading advertising units via Google AdMob and fetching privacy consent forms via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Core gameplay functions fully without an active internet connection.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to monitor network availability before requesting ads, preventing unnecessary network errors and battery drain when playing offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Enables tactile feedback for timing hits, locks, and near-misses. Can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

Required by Google Play Services to access the Google Advertising ID for ad serving, frequency capping, fraud detection, and analytics in compliance with Google Play policy.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Used strictly to deliver the optional 9:00 AM daily challenge reminder notification. This notification is entirely opt-in, stays quiet if you already played that day, goes dormant after 14 days of inactivity, and can be disabled anytime in Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used to re-arm the inexact daily 9:00 AM alarm after a device reboot if you have the daily reminder enabled.

4 Third-Party Advertising & Google UMP Consent

To support continuous development and keep Stillpoint free for everyone, we show advertisements served by Google AdMob.

  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed on the Home and Result screens only; never during active gameplay or pause states.
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed occasionally when navigating away from the Result screen (never during the first 2 runs, at most 1 per 3 runs, and spaced at least 90 seconds apart).
  • Rewarded Ads: Completely optional video ads triggered exclusively when you opt into “ONE MORE TRY” after a run ends (once per run; unavailable in Daily Run to maintain competitive fairness).
  • Native Ads: Labelled ad units displayed within the pause menu.

Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP address, and app diagnostics in accordance with Google's privacy policies:

Google Mobile Ads & Privacy

AdMob uses pseudonymous identifiers to provide frequency capping, fraud prevention, and personalized or non-personalized advertisements based on your consent choices.

5 Consent Revocation & User Privacy Choices

We integrate the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK certified by the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent dialogue upon first launch.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your consent preferences at any time. Simply tap the Settings icon in Stillpoint and select “Ad privacy choices” to reopen the Google UMP consent dialogue and update your selections.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Stillpoint is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older (or 16 in the European Union). It is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our third-party ad providers, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.

7 Global Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

We actively comply with global privacy frameworks:

  • GDPR & UK GDPR: Lawful processing is based on user consent (for personalized advertising) or legitimate interest (for fraud prevention, security, and app stability). You retain the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, or withdraw consent at any time via in-game settings.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA): California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising via the in-game consent settings. We do not sell personal data for monetary consideration.
  • Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP): Handled consistently with European GDPR standards.
