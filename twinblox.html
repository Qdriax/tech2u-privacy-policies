1Introduction & Core Philosophy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Twinblox: Memory Match 3D ("Twinblox", "the application", or "we"), published by Tech2U Savannah. We believe games should provide delightful, transparent, and respectful experiences.

Core Promise: Twinblox does not require player registration, login credentials, phone numbers, or email addresses. Your game saves, scores, win streaks, and unlocked tile skins are stored entirely on your device and are never sold or transmitted to private servers.

2Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

In accordance with data minimization principles, Twinblox stores gameplay data locally in device storage:

  • Game Progress & Records: Completed puzzles, high scores, best move counts, and star ratings across Solo, Rush, Daily, and Versus modes.
  • Cosmetics & Milestones: Unlocked tile skins (e.g. Mint Mallow, Ocean Pop, Brass Toy) earned through game milestones.
  • Preferences: Sound effects, ambient music, color-blind mode markers, reduced motion toggles, and graphic quality.

You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by tapping "Reset progress" in the in-game Settings sheet or by clearing application storage in Android system settings.

3Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

Twinblox requests only the minimal technical permissions required for game operations:

android.permission.VIBRATE

Delivers subtle physical haptic feedback when tapping tiles, flipping matching pairs, and completing levels. Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the application to fetch privacy consent dialogs and display non-intrusive advertisements via the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

4Advertising & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Twinblox is free to play and supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob. To respect player focus and comply strictly with the Google Play Better Ads Experiences Policy:

  • No Mid-Game Interrupts: We never display full-screen ads during active puzzle matching, tile flipping, or timer countdowns.
  • No Menu Interrupts: Full-screen ads never appear when navigating menus, toggling settings, or selecting skins.
  • Natural Milestones: Occasional full-screen ads appear only after a game round finishes on the Result Screen, protected by a 25-second startup grace period and a 45-second cooldown buffer.
  • Opt-In Rewarded Ads: Rewarded video ads are strictly 100% voluntary (e.g., optional +15s time boost in Rush mode).
  • Anchored Adaptive Banner: Placed safely at the bottom perimeter of the display without covering game tiles.

Google AdMob & Google Play Services

AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and coarse interaction diagnostics for ad serving and fraud detection.

5Consent Revocation & User Privacy Choices

In compliance with European Union GDPR (Article 7(3)), UK GDPR, and Swiss DPA regulations, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form upon first launch.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can withdraw or adjust your consent preferences at any time. Simply open the in-game Settings gear icon and tap the "Privacy consent settings" button to reopen your consent choices.

6Children's Privacy Compliance

Twinblox is designed as a family-friendly casual brain puzzle for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly ensure appropriate remediation.

7Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your region, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what data is processed. As noted, Twinblox processes zero personal data on external servers.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all local statistics and records via the in-game reset button or Android application management.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell player personal information for monetary or other considerations.
