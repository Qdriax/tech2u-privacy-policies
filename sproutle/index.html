1 Overview & Core Principles

Welcome to Sproutle: Match 3 Garden Game (“Sproutle”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), developed by Tech2U Savannah (package name: com.tech2u.sproutle). Sproutle is a relaxed, fair garden-themed swap match-3 puzzle designed around transparency and player trust.

Core Privacy Commitment: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, or financial details. Sproutle has no user accounts, requires no login, features zero in-app purchases, and stores 100% of gameplay progress locally on your device.

2 Data We Collect & What We Do Not Collect

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identity information (PII) like names, emails, phone numbers.
  • No account credentials, passwords, or social media logins.
  • No payment, credit card, or banking details (the game has zero in-app purchases).
  • No precise GPS location data.
  • No biometric, microphone, or camera data.

Data Collected Automatically by Third Parties (Google Mobile Ads)

Sproutle integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. When enabled and permitted by your consent choices, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device and Other Identifiers: Mobile advertising ID (Google AAID), device type, operating system version.
  • App Interaction & Performance Data: Ad views, clicks, app crashes, and diagnostic logs.
  • Coarse (Approximate) Location: Derived strictly from general IP address geolocation for serving regional advertising.

3 On-Device Storage & Backup

All game data—including garden plots unlocked, coins earned, best moves, Sunny Streak progress, and player preferences (sound, music, vibration, hints, reminders)—is stored entirely in your device’s local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / sandboxed app storage).

Offline & Device Portability: Because Sproutle runs offline without a remote server account, players can transfer their progress to a new device using an in-game generated backup code (accessible in Settings > Copy backup code). This backup string is handled entirely by you on your device clipboard and is never transmitted to Tech2U Savannah servers.

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, California, and other compliant regions), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you make your privacy selections via the official Google UMP consent dialog.

5 Fairness by Design & Better Ads Compliance

Sproutle is built around strict ethical principles and Google Better Ads Standards:

  • Zero Gameplay Ads: Ads never appear during puzzle play or active move execution. Nothing interrupts your board.
  • No App Open Ads: You are never forced into an ad before the game loads or before you have played.
  • Strict Interstitial Caps: Interstitials only appear after a completed win, behind a clear ad break announcement card, and only starting after your 5th win. They are strictly spaced at least 3 wins and at least 3 minutes apart, and never within 60 seconds of a rewarded ad.
  • Opt-In Rewarded Ads: Completely optional choices to earn +5 extra moves on the fail card or double coins on the win card.
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed only on quiet navigation screens (Garden home and Settings), never during board gameplay.
  • Muted Ads with Sound Setting: Disabling game sound in Settings also mutes third-party ad audio automatically.

6 Device Permissions

Sproutle requests only minimal, standard technical permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements and verifying UMP privacy consent choices.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the app and AdMob SDK to verify whether an active network connection exists.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly for an optional daily 9:00 AM garden reminder (prompted after the 3rd win, easily toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows rescheduling the player-requested morning reminder if your device restarts.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Powers optional gentle haptic feedback on piece swaps and match clears (can be toggled off anytime in Settings).

Sproutle is a gentle, wholesome garden match-3 puzzle suitable for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is collected by Tech2U Savannah, Sproutle maintains a safe, family-friendly gaming environment.

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time. Simply open Sproutle, tap the Settings (⚙) icon, and tap Ad Choices. This immediately relaunches the official Google Privacy & Consent dialog.

Data Erasure: To wipe all local game data, navigate to your device’s Android Settings > Apps > Sproutle > Storage > Clear Data. You can also re-initialize your device advertising ID in Google Settings > Ads.
