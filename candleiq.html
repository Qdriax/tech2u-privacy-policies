Privacy Policy • Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Tech2U LLC ("we", "our", or "us") develops CandleIQ: Candlestick Patterns (the "Application"), an offline-first educational application dedicated to teaching Japanese candlestick anatomy, reversal and continuation patterns, price action trading principles, and risk management.

This Privacy Policy outlines how CandleIQ handles data, user privacy, and advertising compliance under international data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU/UK), Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Data Collection & Zero PII Architecture

CandleIQ is engineered with a strict Data Minimization and Zero PII Architecture. All educational course material, 9 comprehensive modules, diagrams, quizzes, and trade planning tools are stored locally on your device.

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to provide names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Personal Financial Data: The in-app Position Sizing Calculator operates entirely offline in memory. No financial balances or account numbers are collected or transmitted.
  • Offline Course Progress: Module completion, quiz scores, and settings are saved locally using device storage (SharedPreferences). You can clear this anytime using "Reset Progress" in Settings.

3. Advertising & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

To keep high-tier candlestick education 100% free with zero paywalls, CandleIQ utilizes Google AdMob to display non-intrusive ads (anchored banners and occasional interstitial transitions between study sessions).

Google UMP SDK & Consent Management

We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK certified by the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2):

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent dialogue prior to ad serving.
  • No personalized ads are served without explicit opt-in consent.
  • On-Demand Consent Revocation (GDPR Article 7(3)): You can modify or withdraw your consent at any time via Settings & Privacy > Privacy Preferences (UMP Consent) inside CandleIQ.

4. Android Permissions & Alarm Policy Compliance

CandleIQ requires only minimal system permissions required for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to load AdMob advertisements and verify connectivity.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively for voluntary 08:30 AM morning trading discipline and market psychology quotes. This can be toggled on or off at any time.
  • Exact Alarm Policy Compliance: CandleIQ strictly avoids requesting SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM or USE_EXACT_ALARM, utilizing inexact background scheduling in compliance with Google Play system battery conservation mandates.

5. Educational Disclaimer & Third-Party Partner Links

CandleIQ is an educational tool designed to teach technical chart analysis. Nothing in the Application constitutes financial, investment, or trading advice. Trading financial instruments, Forex, and CFDs involves substantial risk of loss.

The Application may feature informational links to regulated multi-asset brokers (such as HFM). When you click an external link, you leave CandleIQ and are governed by that third party's privacy policy and terms.
