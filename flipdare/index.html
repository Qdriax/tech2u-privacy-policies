1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to FlipDare (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed by Tech2U Savannah. FlipDare is a casual physics trick-shot bottle flip puzzle and scene builder designed around offline-first autonomy, zero server tracking, and complete privacy.

Zero-Server Architecture: FlipDare operates entirely without user accounts, logins, telemetry tracking, or external database servers. All bottle-flip simulations, level solving, custom scene codes, and replay clips execute 100% locally on your device.

2 Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

FlipDare stores gameplay state strictly on your local device sandbox:

  • Tutorial progress and solver-verified completion records (Scenes 1–20).
  • Local scene creations and custom share codes (generated via self-describing checksummed payloads).
  • Local unlocked bottle skins and daily challenge completion streaks.
  • Audio volume, haptic vibration, and reduced motion accessibility settings.
  • Ad pacing counters (to enforce non-intrusive ad intervals).

This data is never transmitted to any external server and never linked to any personally identifiable information (PII).

3 Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

FlipDare requests only minimal permissions necessary for app operation:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used solely to query Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent and load ads when connected to the internet.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the game to gracefully detect connectivity and operate seamlessly offline without unnecessary network attempts.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic feedback during flips, bounces, and clean landings. Can be completely disabled in Settings.

4 Third-Party Advertising & Google UMP Consent

FlipDare uses Google AdMob to deliver ads. In compliance with global privacy regulations (including EU/UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and US State Privacy Laws):

  • Pre-Consent Enforcement: AdMob is never initialized and no ad requests are made until consent is gathered via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.
  • Ad Pacing Ethics: Interstitial ads are shown only at natural boundaries (never during active flips or building) and are strictly throttled (minimum 8 attempts and 90 seconds between impressions).
  • Rewarded Ads: Fully voluntary and opt-in for bonus cosmetic previews or HD clip exports.

5 Consent Revocation & Opt-Out Rights

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FlipDare is designed as a family-friendly physics arcade puzzle suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately for prompt remediation.

7 Global Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

FlipDare complies with global data privacy frameworks:

  • GDPR / UK GDPR: Lawful basis for any ad processing relies on explicit consent via certified CMP. You have the right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • CCPA / CPRA: We do not sell or share personal information. California residents may exercise opt-out preferences via the in-app privacy menu.
