1 Overview & Core Principles

Popsprout ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a relaxing tap-to-blast garden puzzle game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.popsprout).

Offline-First & Fair By Design: Popsprout requires NO user accounts, NO cloud sign-ins, NO phone number or email collection, and NO background tracking. All puzzle progression, stars, garden blooms, coins, and backup codes remain strictly stored on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, contacts, or biometric data.

Any technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to deliver compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Popsprout saves your progress locally using sandboxed device storage (Android SharedPreferences key save_v1):

  • Level progress across 120 handcrafted garden levels and earned star ratings.
  • Garden blooms and visual growth state (up to 20 garden plots).
  • Coin balances and booster inventories (Trowel, Sprinkler, Sunbeam).
  • Audio and notification settings preferences.
  • A self-contained, checksummed backup code (PS1- prefix) that lets you transfer progress to another device without cloud logins.

This data stays 100% on your device and can be erased anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Popsprout > Storage > Clear Data.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Popsprout is supported by unobtrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~4638321722). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Popsprout strictly adhere to Google Better Ads Standards and our "Fair by Design" promise:

  • Zero Gameplay Ads: There are NEVER any ads during active puzzle play, at level start, or immediately upon pressing next level.
  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choices on the win or loss dialogs to earn bonus rewards or an extra second-wind move.
  • Interstitial Ads: Strictly capped transitions shown only after a successful win (starting from Level 10 onwards, spaced at least 3 wins and at least 3 minutes apart). Never shown after a loss.
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed only on quiet navigation menus (Home garden and Level selection bottom sheet).

5 Device Permissions

Popsprout requests only essential technical permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements and verifying UMP consent.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly for an optional daily 9:00 AM garden reminder (prompted once after your first win, easily disabled in Settings).

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows rescheduling the optional local morning notification if the device restarts, only if enabled by the player.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Popsprout is a gentle, wholesome tap-to-blast garden puzzle designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is requested or collected, Popsprout offers a safe, clean gaming environment.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time by opening the in-game Settings menu and tapping Ad & Privacy Choices. This immediately relaunches Google's official privacy preferences form.

To delete all locally stored game data, go to Android Settings > Apps > Popsprout > Storage > Clear Data.
