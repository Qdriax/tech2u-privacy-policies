1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Topheavy: Overloaded Truck (package name: com.tech2u.topheavy).

Core Guarantee: Topheavy is built strictly on privacy-by-design principles. We do not require account creation, login credentials, cloud accounts, or social media linking. All career progress, truck unlocks, upgrades, high score records, and settings remain stored solely on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, telephone number, or contacts.

Any telemetry or technical identifiers required for ad serving, fraud prevention, and consent verification are handled strictly by certified third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform) as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Topheavy stores your game progress locally within the sandboxed application environment:

  • Coin balances and unlocked delivery trucks (Tuk Tuk, Pickup, Matatu, Bush Jeep, Old Lorry).
  • Truck mechanical upgrades (Engine, Springs, and Rope).
  • Vehicle paint selections and visual preferences.
  • Career level, route completion, stars earned, tallest pile delivered, and Greedy Run high scores.
  • Daily challenge seed progress and daily login streaks.
  • Player settings: Sound effects, Music volume, Haptic vibration, and Morning Reminder toggle.

This data never leaves your device and can be permanently erased at any time by clearing application data in Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP & AdMob)

Topheavy is free to play and supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob. We implement Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply fully with global privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In regions where consent or opt-out is required by law (such as the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, and relevant US states), no ad requests or advertising identifiers are processed until you have submitted your preferences via Google's certified UMP consent form.

Advertisements follow Google Play Better Ads policies and include:

  • Opt-in Rewarded Ads: Entirely optional ads that allow you to double your delivery payout on the score screen or recover a spilled cargo load after a failed run. You choose whether and when to watch them.
  • Interstitials: Infrequent interstitial ads displayed strictly after a completed delivery score screen behind a clear "Ad break" notification, with strict cooldown timers (never during cargo loading or driving).
  • Adaptive Banners: Non-intrusive banners displayed only on quiet menu screens (Home, Garage, Jobs, and Settings) and completed score screens.

5 Device Permissions

Topheavy requests only the minimum permissions necessary for gameplay, haptics, and ad operations:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ads and display Google UMP privacy consent dialogues.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by the network stack to check whether an active internet connection is available.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Powers responsive physical haptic feedback when dropping cargo, hitting humps, or rolling over (can be toggled off at any time in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used solely if you choose to enable the optional 9:00 AM Daily Load challenge reminder notification. You can enable or disable this at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

6 Optional Morning Reminder

If you toggle on the morning reminder in Settings, Topheavy schedules a local notification for 9:00 AM local time reminding you that a new Daily Load manifest is ready. This notification is scheduled purely locally on your device without sending any personal data to external servers. You can turn it off anytime with one tap in Settings.

7 Children’s Privacy & Family Policy

Topheavy is a lighthearted 3D balance and cargo stacking game featuring cartoonish vehicle physics with zero human injury, blood, or violence. The game is designed for all audiences and complies with Google Play Families Policy standards. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe any minor has provided data, contact us for immediate remediation.

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under international privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you have full authority over your data:

  • Consent Revocation at Any Time: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game by opening Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Data Erasure: All delivery progress and saved coins live on your device. Clear the app storage in your Android settings to immediately wipe all data.
