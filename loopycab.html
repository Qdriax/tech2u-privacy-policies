1Introduction & Core Philosophy

This Privacy Policy explains how Loopy Cab ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed and distributed by Tech2U Savannah (package: com.tech2u.loopycab), manages player data and privacy.

We believe great arcade gaming should be transparent, lightweight, and respectful of user privacy. Loopy Cab operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, or access to sensitive device sensors.

2Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

The app only stores gameplay information strictly on your local device storage:

  • Match Statistics & Records: Completed taxi shifts, fares collected, perfect pickups, streak multiplier, high scores (Day Shift, Rush Hour, Daily Route), and crash records.
  • Cosmetic Customization: Unlocked vehicle skins (Checker Cab, Delivery Van, Police Interceptor, Hot Rod, Sports Car, etc.) and active car selections.
  • Settings & Preferences: Audio volume, haptic feedback toggle, colour-blind mode (stripes on cars), reduce motion mode, graphics rendering fidelity, and local daily notification preferences.

This data is stored solely in local app sandbox storage and can be erased instantly at any time via the in-game "Reset" option or Android Application Manager.

3Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to fetch non-intrusive advertisements from Google AdMob and verify network connectivity status.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely for scheduling the optional daily route reminder (9:00 AM local time). Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic feedback when changing lanes, easing off the pedal, picking up fares, or scraping traffic. Can be toggled off in Settings.

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

Used by Google Play Services to provide compliant advertising identifiers according to user consent selections.

4Advertising & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Loopy Cab is free to play and supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob. To respect player focus and comply strictly with the Google Play Better Ads Experiences Policy:

  • No Mid-Game Interrupts: We never display full-screen ads during active roundabout driving, fare pickups, or lane changes.
  • No Menu Interrupts: Full-screen ads never appear when navigating menus, toggling settings, or selecting cars in the garage.
  • Natural Milestones & Cooldown: Occasional full-screen ads appear only after a shift ends on the Result Screen (first interstitial requires 3 completed runs, then at most one per 3 completed runs, with a strict minimum 90-second cooldown buffer).
  • Opt-In Rewarded Ads: Rewarded video ads are strictly 100% voluntary (e.g. continuing a qualifying run or test-driving a locked car in the garage for one shift).
  • Anchored Adaptive Banner: Displayed safely at the bottom perimeter of menu screens only and automatically hidden during active gameplay.

Google AdMob & Google Play Services

AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and coarse interaction diagnostics for ad serving and fraud detection.

5Consent Revocation & User Privacy Choices

In compliance with European Union GDPR (Article 7(3)), UK GDPR, and Swiss DPA regulations, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form upon first launch.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can withdraw or adjust your consent preferences at any time. Simply open the in-game Settings screen and tap the "Privacy Preferences" button to reopen your consent choices.

6Children's Privacy Compliance

Loopy Cab is designed as a family-friendly arcade driving game for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly ensure appropriate remediation.

7Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your region, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what data is processed. Loopy Cab processes zero personal data on external servers.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all local statistics and records via the in-game reset button or Android application management.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell player personal information for monetary or other considerations.
