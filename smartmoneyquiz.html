1Introduction & Offline-First Architecture

Tech2U Savannah ("we", "our", or "us") develops Smart Money Quiz (Package ID: com.tech2u.smartmoneyquiz). We believe privacy is an absolute priority. Smart Money Quiz is engineered as an offline-first, zero-account educational companion.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Smart Money Quiz does not require account creation, logins, or submission of personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or brokerage account details. All 14 course modules, 84 quiz questions, and study scores remain stored strictly on your device.

2Information We Do Not Collect

To preserve complete trader privacy, we enforce strict data minimization:

  • No Account Creation: No username, passwords, or personal profiles are collected or required.
  • No Financial or Trading Data: We never request or access broker credentials, trade histories, API keys, or financial account balances.
  • No Payment Information: The application is completely free to access with zero in-app purchases or payment processing.
  • No Precise Geolocation: We never request access to GPS or fine location hardware.

3Local Data Storage & Progress Tracking

The app stores non-sensitive configuration and learning progress entirely on your local device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Completed Modules & Reading Progress: Tracks which of the 14 SMC modules have been studied.
  • Quiz Scores & Pass History: Records your multiple-choice quiz marks to display mastery analytics.
  • Study Reminder Preferences: Stores your daily 8:30 AM push notification preference and last-seen morning briefing date.

You can reset all stored study progress at any time via Settings → Reset Course Progress.

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required exclusively for requesting digital advertising via the Google Mobile Ads SDK and querying GDPR consent preferences via Google UMP.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to deliver optional daily 8:30 AM study notifications. This permission can be toggled on or off at any time inside the app's Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & VIBRATE

Used to reschedule daily 8:30 AM study reminders locally after a device reboot without connecting to external servers.

5Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

Smart Money Quiz is supported by digital advertising via Google AdMob. To comply with GDPR, UK GDPR, and Google Play policies:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): On first launch in relevant jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app presents a Google-certified consent form. Users can accept, reject, or customize ad personalization.
  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: In full compliance with GDPR Article 7(3), users can change or withdraw their consent preferences at any time by navigating to Settings → Privacy Consent Preferences.
  • Ad-Free Learning Zones: In-flight lesson reading and active quiz question screens are 100% ad-free.

6Financial & Trading Risk Disclaimer

Educational Revision Companion Only

Smart Money Quiz is an educational revision companion designed to teach market structure, liquidity concepts, and institutional order flow theory. This application does not provide investment advice, trading signals, financial recommendations, or brokerage facilities.

Trading foreign exchange, equities, commodities, and derivatives carries substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for all investors. Never trade with capital you cannot afford to lose.

7Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Smart Money Quiz is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

8Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you have statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Rectification: All study progress is stored locally on your device and accessible directly within the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can wipe all local application data using the "Reset Course Progress" option in Settings or by clearing application storage in Android Settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell user personal information. Personalized ads can be opted out of via the in-app consent preferences menu.
