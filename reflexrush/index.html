1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Reflex Rush: Home Workout Game ("the App", package com.tech2u.reflexrush) is developed and published by Tech2U Savannah ("we", "us", or "our"). Reflex Rush is an offline-first, camera-scored body-motion exergame featuring three mini-games (Cue Rush, Match the Outline, and Whack-a-Mole) plus a camera-free Follow Mode.

Private by Design: We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles. All camera pose estimation runs locally on your phone in real time. Video frames are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

2 On-Device Camera & Pose Processing

When you play in Camera mode, Reflex Rush uses your device’s front camera and Google ML Kit Pose Detection (on-device) to read body landmarks in real time:

  • No Video Recording or Storage: Camera frames are analyzed transiently in memory to score your moves and are immediately discarded.
  • No Server Uploads: Neither camera video nor body landmark coordinates ever leave your device.
  • Share Cards: When you share a session result card, the App generates a stylized stick-figure graphic with your score—it never includes camera footage or photos.
  • Camera-Free Option: You may decline camera access and play every session in Follow Mode.

3 Information Stored Locally

Your gameplay history and preferences are stored strictly within your device’s private application sandbox (SQLite and SharedPreferences):

  • Session History & Progress: Session scores, accuracy, average reaction time, estimated calories, streaks, and personal bests.
  • Settings & Preferences: Theme choice, difficulty, Small Space mode, Follow Mode default, voice cues, sound effects, haptics, and morning reminder time.

We do not operate backend servers and never transmit your gameplay history off your device.

4 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Reflex Rush is free to play and supported by advertising via Google AdMob (Google LLC). No ads are ever shown during calibration or active gameplay. When an internet connection is available and consent requirements are satisfied, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device or Other IDs: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app-set identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Diagnostics & Approximate Location: Basic crash/performance diagnostics and coarse IP-derived region for ad relevance and compliance.

You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5 Consent Management & Revocation (Google UMP)

Reflex Rush integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Before initializing Mobile Ads or requesting any ad, the App queries your consent status and presents a consent form where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland).

You can review, change, or withdraw your ad consent preferences at any time by opening Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the App.

6 Device Permissions

android.permission.CAMERA

Used only during calibration and active Camera-mode gameplay for on-device pose detection. Optional—Follow Mode works without it.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used solely to check connectivity and load Google AdMob ads and UMP consent forms. Core gameplay works fully offline.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used only if you enable the optional Morning Reminder in Settings, and to reschedule that local reminder after a device restart.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides optional haptic feedback when hitting cues and targets.

7 Health & Safety Disclaimer

Reflex Rush is an entertainment and movement game, not a medical device or medical advice. Calorie figures are activity-level estimates only. Clear the space around you, play on a non-slip floor, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

8 Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Reflex Rush is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured with a maximum ad content rating of PG.

9 Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Swiss DPA)

We comply with the EU/UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California CCPA/CPRA. Because we do not operate user accounts or collect personal data on our own servers, your local gameplay data remains under your direct control. For ad-related processing by Google, you may exercise your rights via the in-app Ad privacy choices option or by contacting us.

10 Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all local session history, streaks, and personal bests at any time via Settings → Clear history, or by uninstalling the App.
