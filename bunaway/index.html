1 Overview & Core Principles

Bunaway ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a fast-paced 3D shape-shifting bakery arcade game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.bunaway).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Bunaway requires NO accounts, NO cloud sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO external player tracking. All gameplay progress, best escape batches, unlocked dough skins, streaks, and customization options remain strictly stored on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, device phone number, or contacts.

Any technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to deliver compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Bunaway saves your game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Escape progression: current batch, completed escapes, win rate, best time, and highest batch reached.
  • Scores and records across game modes: Great Escape, Daily Batch, Rolling Pin, and Dough Duel.
  • Unlocked dough skins: Gingerbread, Matcha Melon, Bear Claw, Thick Toast, Burnt End, and more.
  • Lifetime stats: total runs, streak records, hairlines cleared, butter collected, and duel replays.
  • Player settings: sound volume, music shuffle, haptic feedback, color-blind mode, reduced motion, low quality graphics, and daily reminder preference.

This data stays 100% on your device and can be erased anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Bunaway > Storage > Clear Data.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Bunaway is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~5852824336). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Bunaway strictly adhere to Google Better Ads Standards and never interrupt active racing:

  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choices: (a) "Keep Rolling" to revive once per Rolling Pin run, or (b) "Head Start" to gain an initial burst after a lost Great Escape race.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only as a finished run ends (every 2nd run, never on the first run of a session, at least 90s apart, 2 minutes after any rewarded ad, and never after achieving a new personal best).
  • App Open Ads: Displayed only upon returning to the app after 30+ minutes away, never on cold starts or during active runs.
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed only on quiet menu screens (Home, Pantry/Skins, Scoreboard, Settings). Never shown during conveyor gameplay or on the results panel.

5 Device Permissions

Bunaway requests only essential technical permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements and verifying UMP consent.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to verify network connectivity for ad serving and offline mode.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic feedback when morphing through cutters and collecting butter (toggleable in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly for an optional daily 9:00 AM bakery reminder (prompted on Android 13+, easily disabled in Settings).

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows rescheduling the daily morning reminder if the device restarts, only if enabled by the user.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Bunaway is an arcade puzzle runner designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is requested or collected, Bunaway offers a safe, family-friendly gaming experience.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time by opening the in-game Settings screen and tapping Privacy Settings. This immediately relaunches Google's official privacy preferences form.

To delete all locally stored game data, go to Android Settings > Apps > Bunaway > Storage > Clear Data.
