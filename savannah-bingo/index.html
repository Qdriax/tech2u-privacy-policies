1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Savannah Bingo: Safari Game (package name: com.tech2u.savannahbingo).

Core Guarantee: Savannah Bingo is built on a strict privacy-by-design and fair-play philosophy. We require no accounts, no logins, no cloud registrations, and no social media linking. All stamp collection progress, stars, settings, and round statistics remain strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Any telemetry or technical identifiers required for ad serving and consent verification are handled strictly by certified third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform) as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Savannah Bingo saves your progress locally within the sandboxed application environment (via HTML5 localStorage):

  • Completed safari destination stamps and stamp album unlock milestones.
  • Accumulated star balance earned from daubing numbers and completing bingos.
  • Audio and gameplay preferences: Ball speed (Relaxed, Normal, Quick), Auto-daub toggle, Voice caller toggle, and Sound effects toggle.
  • Optional daily reminder notification preference.

This data never leaves your device and can be permanently erased at any time by clearing application data in your Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP & AdMob)

Savannah Bingo is supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob. We implement Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply fully with global privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In regions where consent or opt-out is required by law (such as the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, and relevant US states), no ad requests or advertising identifiers are processed until you have submitted your preferences via Google's UMP consent form.

Our strict ad delivery standards ensure fair, uninterrupted gameplay:

  • No Ads During Rounds: No banner or interstitial ads are ever displayed while a bingo round is active or on the live round screen.
  • Paced Interstitials: Interstitials appear only on tapping Continue after the completed results screen, with a minimum frequency cap of every 2nd round.
  • Quiet Banners: Small banners are displayed only on menu and album screens.
  • App Open Ads: Paced with a minimum 4-hour window, only on second session onward, and never displayed while inside an active round.

5 Device Permissions

Savannah Bingo requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ads and display Google UMP privacy consent dialogues.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Requested on Android 13+ solely if you choose to enable the optional 9:00 AM daily safari reminder in Settings.

6 Optional Morning Safari Reminder

If you toggle on the daily reminder in Settings, Savannah Bingo schedules a local WorkManager notification for approximately 9:00 AM local time. This reminder is scheduled purely locally on your device without sending any personal data to external servers. You can turn it off at any time with a single tap in the in-game Settings menu.

7 No Real Money or Gambling Disclosures

Savannah Bingo is strictly a casual, single-player arcade game for entertainment purposes only:

  • You play against computer rival players.
  • There is no real money gaming, no wagering, no deposit mechanic, and no opportunity to win real currency, cash prizes, or real-world rewards.
  • There are no in-app purchases or paid currency packs. Nothing in the game is sold for real money.
  • Success in this game does not imply future success in real-money gambling.

8 Children’s Privacy & Family Policy

Savannah Bingo is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take corrective action.

9 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under international privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, FADP), you maintain full authority over your data:

  • Consent Revocation at Any Time: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game by tapping Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Data Erasure: All game data resides locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local records.
