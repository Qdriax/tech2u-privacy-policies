1Overview & Offline-First Core

Orbit Out: Spin Shatter Escape (package: com.pulselab.orbitout), published by Tech2U Savannah ("we", "our", or "us"), is an original offline physics-based ring rotation and escape arcade game for Android devices.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Orbit Out does not require any account creation, user registration, email address, or cloud login. All game progression, medal ratings, and theme unlocks reside exclusively on your local device.

2Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Your GPS location or coarse location data.
  • Your contacts, microphone audio, camera feeds, photos, or media files.
  • Your biometric data, financial accounts, or payment credentials.

3Local Data Storage & Progress

The game saves your progress using Android's private SharedPreferences storage. This data includes:

  • Campaign level progression (Levels 1–60) and medal ratings (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
  • Earned stars and unlocked visual world themes.
  • Endless mode high score and daily challenge streak records.
  • Sound and haptic feedback settings preferences.

This data is never sent to our servers and is immediately deleted when you uninstall the game or clear app storage in your Android system settings.

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

Orbit Out requests only the absolute minimum permissions required for gameplay mechanics and compliant ad rendering:

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during ball bounces, ring shatters, nudges, and spike collisions. Can be toggled off at any time in Settings.

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading advertisements via Google AdMob and checking consent status via Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the Google Mobile Ads SDK to monitor network reachability before attempting ad requests.

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

Declared for Google Play services ad serving and frequency capping, managed strictly according to user consent choices.

5Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

Orbit Out is free to play and supported by non-intrusive full-screen advertisements (interstitials between levels and optional rewarded revives). There are zero banner ads obscuring gameplay.

GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss DPA & US Privacy Compliance: We integrate the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Before any ad is requested or initialized, users in applicable jurisdictions are presented with a consent form specifying choices regarding personalized vs. non-personalized advertising.

Third-Party Partner: Google AdMob

Advertising services are provided by Google LLC. Google may process device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and your consent choices.

6Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Orbit Out does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not contain mature themes, violence, or user communications, and conforms to Google Play Family Policy guidelines.

7User Rights, Consent Revocation & Data Deletion

Users have the right to access, rectify, or delete their local game data at any time:

  • Consent Revocation: In the game's Settings screen, tap CONSENT PREFERENCES to modify or withdraw your ad consent choices at any time.
  • Data Reset: In Settings, double-tap RESET PROGRESS to completely clear all locally stored stars, medals, streaks, and unlocks.
  • Full Deletion: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local storage.
