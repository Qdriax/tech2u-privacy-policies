1Introduction & Core Architecture

Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us"), a software division of QDRIAX NOURIX, develops and operates PipClock: Forex Market Hours (Application ID: com.tech2u.pipclock). We hold personal privacy as a fundamental human right. PipClock is engineered as an offline-first market timing and reference utility that runs complex session reconciliation and daylight saving calculations locally on your mobile device.

Core Privacy Invariant: PipClock does not require account registration, requests no passwords, collects zero banking or trading credentials, and maintains zero private telemetry servers.

2Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Under no circumstances do we collect, harvest, store, or sell:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • Broker or Trading Credentials: No MT4/MT5 passwords, investor passwords, trading balances, or API tokens.
  • Precise Physical Location: Zero GPS, cellular triangulation, or nearby Wi-Fi network scanning. Time zones are inferred purely from your phone's standard OS clock offset.
  • Media & Device Content: Zero access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage files.

3Local Data Storage & Preferences

All user customization is saved exclusively on your device within the secure sandboxed SharedPreferences storage:

  • Selected retail broker profile (e.g. HFM, IC Markets, Exness, XM, FP Markets, Pepperstone).
  • Active clock context preference (Local phone time, Broker server time, or New York anchor time).
  • 12-hour or 24-hour time display format preference.
  • Visual theme preference (Dark Mode, Light Mode, or automatic System Default).
  • Custom user-added economic events and alert lead-time intervals.
  • Notification scheduling toggles (Daily 07:30 AM market briefing, session opens, and weekend warnings).

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

PipClock requests only the minimum Android permissions strictly required for its intended utilities:

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Network Access

Required exclusively to initialize the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK and load Google AdMob advertisements.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

In-Tray Notifications (Android 13+)

Allows the app to display scheduled alerts in your notification tray, including the daily 07:30 AM morning session briefing and user-configured economic release reminders.

android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM

Exact Session Timing

Required to wake the notification engine at the exact second market sessions open (e.g. London 07:00 UTC, NY 12:00 UTC) or 15 minutes before high-impact economic news releases.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Reboot Restoration

Ensures scheduled reminders (such as your daily 07:30 AM briefing) are accurately rescheduled when your phone restarts, without needing to open the app.

5Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

PipClock is a 100% free application funded entirely through digital advertisements served by Google AdMob. We comply with all global privacy regulations (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and CCPA/CPRA):

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): On first launch in regulated territories (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and California), users are presented with a certified consent form to select personalized or non-personalized ad options.
  • AdMob SDK Integration: AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and app interaction data solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping.
  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via More → Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent Preferences.

9Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Rectify: You can review and modify your app settings and calendar events at any time inside the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Uninstalling the application or clearing app data in Android settings instantly erases all locally stored preferences.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell user personal information. CCPA/CPRA opt-out choices can be toggled via the Privacy Preferences menu.
