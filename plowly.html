1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile arcade game Plowly: Snow Plow Rescue Game (package name: com.tech2u.plowly).

Core Guarantee: Plowly is built with a strict privacy-first foundation. We do not require accounts, sign-ins, social profiles, email addresses, or cloud registration. All gameplay progress, high scores, unlocked plow trucks, logbook statistics, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Any technical data transmission is strictly limited to certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) necessary to serve compliant advertisements and manage user consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Plowly saves game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • High scores across Night Shift, Daily Storm, and Whiteout Blitz modes.
  • Logbook statistics: total stranded cars rescued, square meters of snow plowed, longest rescue chains, and career shifts driven.
  • Unlocked plow trucks (Mail Mint, Fire Chief, Big Grader, Farm Tractor, Ice Cream, Midnight, and Gold Plow).
  • Accessibility and gameplay settings: engine sound effects, haptic vibrations, colour-blind pattern rings and bold timers, reduced motion, low quality graphics setting, and daily morning reminder state.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing application data in your Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Plowly is supported by player-friendly mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~9026991437). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized advertising identifiers or ad requests are initialized until you have reviewed and submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Plowly strictly adhere to Google's Better Ads Experiences rules (ads never appear during active snow plowing or rescue gameplay):

  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choices: (a) "Dig it out" to clear one buried car strike in Night Shift once per shift, or (b) test-drive any locked truck in the Garage for one shift.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only when leaving a shift result screen (never in the first 2 shifts of a session, at most every 2nd finished shift, at least 2 minutes apart, never after a new personal best, and never within 2 minutes of a rewarded ad).
  • App Open Ads: Shown only on return to an idle Home screen after 4+ hours away, never on cold starts or in the first 2 app launches.
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively in an isolated strip below non-gameplay screens (Home, Logbook, and Garage). Never displayed during active plowing shifts.
  • Family Safe Ad Rating: All ad requests are configured with MaxAdContentRating.g to ensure only universally appropriate creative content is loaded.

5 Device Permissions

Plowly requests only the minimal technical permissions required for game features:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad inventory and load privacy consent forms via Google AdMob and Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to detect active network connectivity before sending requests.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Enables tactile haptic feedback when steering through snow banks and rescuing stranded cars (can be toggled off anytime in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you explicitly opt-in to the 09:00 AM Morning Reminder for new Daily Storm snow runs. Permission is requested only after your first finished shift and can be turned on or off anytime in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Plowly is a wholesome arcade game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly target, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). All advertising is rated G. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure appropriate protections or data purging.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection regulations, you retain full control over your privacy:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your consent choices at any time directly inside the game by opening Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing application storage in your Android settings.
