1Overview & Offline-First Philosophy

Void Rush: Black Hole Arcade (package: com.hypergame.voidrush), developed by Tech2U Savannah ("we", "our", or "us"), is an original offline neon-arcade physics game designed for Android devices.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Void Rush requires zero account creation, zero email registration, and zero cloud sign-in. All gameplay records, high scores, lifetime star counts, and cosmetic achievements reside exclusively on your local device.

2Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, sell, share, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including:

  • Your legal name, email address, home address, or phone number.
  • Your GPS location, Wi-Fi location, or coarse location data.
  • Your contacts, phonebook, microphone audio, camera feeds, photos, or personal files.
  • Your financial credentials, credit cards, or banking information.

3Local Data Storage & High Scores

Void Rush saves gameplay data locally on your device via Android's private, sandboxed SharedPreferences storage. This includes:

  • Local high score and survival timer records.
  • Lifetime stars collected and bonus multipliers.
  • Sound effects and haptic vibration preferences.

This data is strictly confined to your device's sandbox. It is never transmitted to Tech2U Savannah servers and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the application or clear app storage via Android system settings.

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

Void Rush requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary for arcade gameplay, tactile feedback, and optional morning engagement reminders:

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used to provide responsive tactile haptic pulses when gravitationally capturing stars, absorbing cosmic debris, and entering event horizon singularities. Can be toggled at any time.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to verify consent status and deliver compliant advertisements.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

On Android 13+ (API 33+), permits an optional morning in-tray reminder notification (scheduled daily at 08:30 AM local time) to keep your cosmic high score streak active. Users may disable notification permissions at any time via Android app notification settings.

5Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

Void Rush is a 100% free-to-play arcade game supported by Google AdMob (non-intrusive Banners, Frequency-Capped Interstitials, and Opt-In Rewarded Ads for extra revive time or double scores). We rigorously enforce global privacy regulations (EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and US CCPA/CPRA):

  • Consent-First Initialization: Ads SDK initialization is gated behind the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Advertising requests are withheld until required consent states are satisfied.
  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: Players can adjust or revoke advertising consent at any time via the PRIVACY button located on the main menu, which immediately displays the Google UMP consent dialogue.
  • Opt-In Rewarded Ads: Rewarded ads are strictly optional and only trigger when explicitly requested by the player to revive or multiply round score.

Third-Party Service Providers

6Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Void Rush is a stylized, non-violent arcade game designed for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), all ad requests comply with child-directed treatment declarations where required.

7User Rights, Consent Revocation & Data Deletion

Under international privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, LGPD), you maintain complete authority over your device data:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Tap PRIVACY on the game title screen anytime to reopen your consent choices.
  • Right to Opt Out of Ad Tracking: Reset or delete your Google Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Right to Erasure: Reset your score by clearing application storage in Android Settings → Apps → Void Rush → Storage → Clear Data.
