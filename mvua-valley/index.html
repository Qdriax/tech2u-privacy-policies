1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the Android mobile game Mvua Valley: Match 3 Restore (package name: com.tech2u.mvuavalley).

Core Guarantee: Mvua Valley is built on a strict privacy-first foundation. We do not require account registration, social logins, email sign-ups, or cloud identifiers. Your puzzle progression, stars earned, completed restoration tasks, and game settings remain stored exclusively on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Any technical data processing is handled exclusively through certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform) strictly as required to serve compliant advertisements and record user consent choices as outlined below.

3 On-Device Storage

Mvua Valley saves gameplay state locally on your device via sandboxed local storage (HTML5 localStorage in the embedded Android WebView):

  • Completed puzzle levels (1–36) and star ratings.
  • Restoration progress across the 12 savannah tasks (spring, waterhole, acacias, wildlife).
  • Booster inventory (Hammers, Shuffles) and daily streak records.
  • Audio and accessibility options: sound effects, ambient music, and language selection (English / Kiswahili).
  • Daily Rain morning reminder preference.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you uninstall the app or clear app storage in your Android settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Mvua Valley is supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060). We integrate Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with global privacy standards, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions, no ad requests are initialized and no advertising identifiers are processed until you have submitted your consent preferences via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Mvua Valley strictly respect player experience and Google Play's Better Ads Experiences standards:

  • Rewarded Video: 100% voluntary player choice to earn +5 extra moves on a puzzle or receive a free daily booster.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only after winning a level, starting after level 10, at least every 3 wins and at least 3.5 minutes apart. Never shown before a level or upon losing.
  • No In-App Purchases: The game has no paid microtransactions or consumable loot boxes.

5 Device Permissions

Mvua Valley requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary for gameplay and optional reminders:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ad inventory and fetch privacy consent dialogs via Google AdMob and Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the game to verify network connectivity before requesting ad inventory or launching external links.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Enables crisp tactile haptic feedback during piece matching and power-up explosions.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you explicitly opt in to the gentle 08:30 AM Morning Reminder for the Daily Rain puzzle. This is completely optional and can be turned on or off anytime in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy

Mvua Valley is designed as a casual family-friendly game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe personal information has inadvertently been provided, please reach out to us for immediate resolution.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international privacy laws, you have complete sovereignty over your privacy choices:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your consent preferences at any time from inside the game by tapping Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app or clearing app storage in Android settings permanently erases all progress and cached data.
