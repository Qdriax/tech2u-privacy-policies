1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Haylow: Farm Town Builder (package name: com.tech2u.haylow).

Core Guarantee: Haylow is built with a privacy-first offline design. We do not require user accounts, sign-ins, social profiles, email addresses, or cloud registration, and the game contains no in-app purchases. All farm progress, barn inventory, town layout, and settings remain stored strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location.

Any technical data transmission is strictly limited to certified third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) necessary to serve compliant advertisements and manage user consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Haylow saves game state locally using sandboxed device storage (HTML5 localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Farm layout, planted crops, animal pens, factory queues, Order Board, Market Truck state, coins, XP, and level progress.
  • Barn goods and Tool Shed building materials, plus unlocked land plots and town buildings.
  • Player preferences: sound effects, music, vibration/haptics, and daily 09:00 AM morning reminder toggle.
  • Optional offline backup code generated locally on your device so you can manually transfer your farm to another phone without an account.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy or share your backup code, and it can be deleted at any time by clearing application data in your Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Haylow is supported by player-friendly mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~2800206426). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no advertising requests are initialized until you have reviewed and submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Haylow follow Google's Better Ads Standards:

  • Rewarded Video ("Hurry" & "Double"): Strictly optional player choices to finish a timer early or double a Market Truck / level-up coin reward.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only behind an announced "Ad break" card after the Market Truck leaves or a level-up card closes (never in the first 5 minutes, at least 5 minutes apart, and never within 60 seconds of a rewarded ad).
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed only while a menu/sheet (Orders, Barn, Truck, Build, Menu) is open, never over the active farm.
  • No App-Open Ads: Haylow never shows ads on launch.

5 Device Permissions

Haylow requests only the minimal technical permissions required for core functionality and ad delivery:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad inventory and load privacy consent forms via Google AdMob and Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to detect active network connectivity before sending requests.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you opt in to the optional 09:00 AM daily morning reminder. Permission is requested only after your first level-up and can be toggled off anytime in Menu.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Haylow is a cozy, family-friendly farm and town building game. We do not knowingly target, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will promptly address it.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and international data protection regulations, you retain full control over your privacy:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your consent choices at any time directly inside the game by opening Menu → Privacy Choices.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing application storage in your Android settings.
