1Introduction & Offline-First Privacy Architecture

Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") operates Volatility Breakout: Channel Strategy (Application ID: com.tech2u.lwbreakout). We believe privacy is an intrinsic right. Volatility Breakout is engineered from the ground up as an offline-first educational application. All 5 curriculum modules, 30 diagnostic quiz questions, and 17 high-resolution channel and trade figures are bundled directly inside the APK/AAB package.

Core Privacy Invariant: The app does not maintain any user accounts, requires no login credentials, collects no biometric or financial data, and communicates with zero private servers.

2Information We Do Not Collect

To ensure total transparency, we explicitly declare that we do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Financial or Trading Credentials: No broker API keys, trading account passwords, MT4/MT5 logins, or balance records.
  • Device Identifiers for Tracking: No cross-app advertising IDs or hardware MAC addresses collected by our code.
  • Precise Location Data: Zero GPS, Wi-Fi tri-angulation, or cellular positioning queries.
  • Media & Photos: Zero access to your photo library, camera, microphone, or external storage files.

3Local Data Storage & Progress Tracking

All user state is stored exclusively on your device using Android's native SharedPreferences sandbox:

  • Module completion flags (Modules 01 to 05).
  • Diagnostic quiz scores, percentage marks, and passing thresholds (70%).
  • Sequential module unlocking preference.
  • Theme selection (System Default, Dark Obsidian, Daylight).
  • Morning discipline reminder notification preference (08:30 AM local time).

You can permanently wipe all local progress at any time directly within the app by tapping Reset Progress on the Readiness tab.

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required exclusively for loading Google AdMob advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent forms. The educational reading engine functions completely offline without internet.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Requested on Android 13+ (API 33+) solely to deliver the optional 08:30 AM daily trading discipline study reminder and morning volatility quote. Can be toggled off at any time in Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Required to reschedule your chosen 08:30 AM study reminder alarms locally in Android's AlarmManager after your device restarts.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides gentle haptic feedback during interactive quiz question selection and study reminder notifications.

5Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

Volatility Breakout is funded through non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob. To uphold global privacy regulations (EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and California CCPA/CPRA), we integrate the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Consent Query: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a certified CMP consent dialogue prior to any ad initialization.
  • On-Demand Revocation: You can withdraw or adjust your consent choices at any time inside the app by going to Settings → Privacy Settings (Consent Preferences).
  • Non-Intrusive Monetization: Reading lessons and quiz answering are 100% ad-free. Interstitials are protected by a strict 10-second cooldown throttle.
