5 Device Permissions

Savanna Gold requests only the essential system permissions necessary for functionality:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ads and receive Google UMP consent forms.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify network connectivity.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Requested on Android 13+ solely if you enable the optional 9:00 AM Daily Bonus reminder in Settings.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during reel stops and wins; can be disabled anytime in Settings.

6 Optional Morning Bonus Reminder

If you toggle on the morning reminder in Settings, the app schedules a local notification for 9:00 AM local time to let you know your Daily Wheel and free coin bonuses are ready. This notification is generated entirely on your device via Android's local notification manager; no server or external push service is involved. You can disable it at any time in Settings.
