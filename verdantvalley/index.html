1 Overview & Core Principles

Verdant Valley ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is an offline-first isometric farming and cozy town-building game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.verdantvalley).

Offline-First & Fair By Design: Verdant Valley requires NO user accounts, NO cloud logins, NO email or phone collection, and NO background location tracking. Your valley layout, crops, barn inventory, coins, gems, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, street address, contacts, or biometric data.

Limited technical data (such as advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and approximate IP-based region) may be processed solely by third-party Google services—specifically Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing—as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Verdant Valley saves your progress locally using sandboxed Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences (verdant_valley_save_v2):

  • Isometric town grid buildings, active farm/bakery/kitchen jobs, and elapsed-time offline catch-up timestamps.
  • Coins, gems, XP level (Levels 1–20), barn capacity, and toolbox materials.
  • Audio mute preferences and equipped valley themes.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Verdant Valley > Storage > Clear Data.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Verdant Valley offers 100% optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060). There are zero forced interstitial or banner ads during gameplay. We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, and US CCPA/CPRA.

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), the Google Mobile Ads SDK is not initialized and no ad requests are sent until your consent status has been queried and approved via Google UMP.

5 In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional one-time cosmetic themes and starter packs are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We never see, process, or store your credit card, mobile money, or payment credentials. Purchase tokens are verified locally on-device via Google Play Billing Library to unlock and restore your entitlements.

6 Device Permissions

Verdant Valley requests only the minimum technical permissions required:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used for loading optional AdMob rewarded video ads, verifying UMP consent, and connecting to Google Play Billing.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to check network availability before requesting optional rewarded ads or store product details.

7 Children’s Privacy

Verdant Valley is a wholesome cozy farming simulation designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, erase, or restrict processing of your data and to withdraw advertising consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time inside the app by opening the Boosts tab and tapping Ad & Privacy Choices, which relaunches Google's official privacy options form.
