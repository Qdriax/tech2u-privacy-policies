1Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Tic Tac Toe Arena (Package ID: com.tech2u.tictactoearena), developed by Tech2U Game Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), is an engaging multi-grid tactical board game designed for Android and global mobile platforms. We prioritize user privacy and uphold strict compliance with international data privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR (UK GDPR), Swiss Data Protection Act (DPA), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Fundamental Privacy Standard: Tic Tac Toe Arena is playable offline without mandatory account registration. We do not sell personal information, and any online services (matchmaking, leaderboards) operate with pseudonymous identifiers.

2Information We Collect & Process

We believe in data minimization. The app only processes the data strictly required to maintain gameplay state and provide requested features:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Board win/loss records, coin balances, sound toggles, and unlocked cosmetic skins are stored locally on your device.
  • Pseudonymous Account Data (Optional): If you play online or sign in via Guest or Google Sign-In, a pseudonymous user identifier (UUID / Firebase Auth ID) and chosen display nickname are created to manage match states and global leaderboard scores.
  • Diagnostic & Device Telemetry (Third-Party): Our monetization partner, Google AdMob, and analytics providers may collect non-identifiable technical data such as device model, OS version, approximate coarse location (country or region level), and diagnostic crash statistics to deliver stable ad serving.

Information We Never Collect: We never collect real-world legal names, physical street addresses, phone numbers, contact address books, microphone audio, camera streams, biometric data, or financial payment details.

3Local Storage & Cloud Matchmaking Architecture

The app uses a hybrid offline/cloud architecture:

  • Offline Modes: Solo play against The Clever Fox Dora AI and 2-player Pass & Play operate 100% offline. No telemetry or gameplay moves are transmitted across the network during offline games.
  • Online Multiplayer & Leaderboard: Live random matchmaking and leaderboard rankings communicate securely over HTTPS with Google Firebase Realtime Database. Only match move coordinates and game scores are synced.

4Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

Tic Tac Toe Arena requests only the minimal technical permissions declared in the Android Manifest:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required for online multiplayer matchmaking, global leaderboard rankings, and loading privacy-compliant AdMob advertisements.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to verify internet connectivity before initiating online room matching or ad requests, conserving mobile battery and device data.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Used strictly to deliver the optional daily 08:30 AM morning tactical challenge reminder. You can enable or disable this anytime via device settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows the Android system to restore the scheduled daily 08:30 AM alarm after device reboots without launching background battery-draining services.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Powers optional tactile haptic feedback during piece placements and game completion triggers (can be toggled in Settings).

5Monetization & Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Tic Tac Toe Arena is 100% free to play with no paywalls and no Pro mode lockouts. All grid sizes (3x3, 4x4, 5x5) and game modes are accessible for all users. The application is supported exclusively through advertisements provided by Google AdMob:

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, coarse location, and app performance data in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and user consent preferences.

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on navigation footers.
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed during natural game transitions (match completion, dialog exits).
  • Rewarded Interstitial Ads: Optional ads that grant in-game arena coins upon user request.

6Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent Revocation

In accordance with European and international privacy mandates, Tic Tac Toe Arena integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK:

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a certified IAB TCF consent dialog upon initial startup.
  • Ads are only initialized and loaded after user consent has been queried and resolved.
  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your consent preferences at any time directly inside the app by navigating to Settings > Privacy Policy or via your device's Google Ads privacy preferences.

7Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Tic Tac Toe Arena is designed for general audiences and family-friendly tactical entertainment. We comply strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). If we become aware that personal information has been inadvertently collected from a child, we immediately delete such records.

8Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, you enjoy comprehensive privacy rights over your data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You can request a summary of data associated with your pseudonymous ID.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your display name anytime via Profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can permanently purge your account and records using the in-app deletion button.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for monetary or other consideration under CCPA/CPRA.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never result in service degradation, pricing differences, or gameplay penalties.

9Data Retention & Account Deletion Protocol

We retain gameplay and matchmaking records only for as long as necessary to provide arena services:

  • Self-Service Account Deletion: The app includes a permanent, built-in "Delete Account" option located in Settings > Delete Account. Tapping this purges your cloud profile, match records, and user credentials from Firebase in real time.
  • Local Data Reset: Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored coins, settings, and scores from your device.
  • Manual Erasure Request: You can also email us directly at developer.tech2u@gmail.com with your pseudonymous player ID for complete cloud data erasure within 30 days.
