1 Overview & Offline Educational Architecture

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SMC Masterclass: Smart Money Concepts mobile application (the "Application", "App", or "Service").

The Application is built as a standalone educational tool covering Smart Money Concepts (SMC), market structure, order blocks, imbalances, inducement, liquidity sweeps, and supply/demand zones. All 14 modules, interactive charts, and 84 quiz questions are bundled locally within the app and function completely offline without requiring an account or providing personal data.

2 Financial Non-Intermediation & Educational Disclaimer

Strict Educational Scope: SMC Masterclass is solely an educational and informational tool. It does not provide investment advice, financial signals, portfolio management, or order execution. All content is for educational simulation and training purposes only.

3 Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We do not collect:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Financial information, bank details, or credit card numbers.
  • Biometric identifiers or media from your device.

4 Local Data Storage & Progress

The Application stores course progression state, completed module markers, quiz attempts, and theme preferences locally on your device via standard SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device.

5 Device Permissions & Technical Purpose

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to fetch ad units via Google AdMob and to query user consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the AdMob SDK to determine whether active internet connectivity exists before attempting network calls.

6 Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

The Application is monetized exclusively through advertisements served by Google AdMob. To comply with global privacy regulations (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), the Application implements the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent form on first startup. Users may review or change their consent decisions at any time via the Consent preferences option in the app's More menu.
