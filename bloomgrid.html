1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to BloomGrid: Block Combo Puzzle ("the App"), developed and published by Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting player privacy, providing a transparent gaming experience, and eliminating account friction. BloomGrid is an offline-capable 8x8 block combo puzzle designed to deliver pure strategic fun without invasive surveillance or personal data collection.

Core Guarantee: We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location coordinates, or address book. You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to play.

2 Information Handled Locally

BloomGrid operates primarily using local device storage. The following data is processed and stored exclusively on your device using Android SharedPreferences and browser local storage:

  • Gameplay Progress: Current active board state, current score, high score, and daily challenge streak counter.
  • Boosters: Remaining counts for Undo, Rotate, and New Set actions.
  • Player Preferences: Audio (sound effects), haptic vibration toggle, and preferred visual theme (e.g., Bloom Night, Candy Pop, Deep Ocean, Forest Wood, Neon Grid, Sunset Sky).
  • Local Notifications: Morning reminder toggle preference (managed locally via Android WorkManager).

This data never leaves your device and is permanently erased if you clear application storage or uninstall the App.

3 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing game development and provide BloomGrid completely free of charge, the App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process specific technical telemetry, including:

  • Device and Other Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and hardware model.
  • App Interactions: Ad views, clicks, and interaction timestamps.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Crash logs, app startup duration, and diagnostic telemetry for ad delivery stability.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS).

For detailed information on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google’s Advertising Privacy Policy.

4 Consent Management & Revocation (Google UMP)

BloomGrid complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and US state privacy regulations (including California CCPA/CPRA) by incorporating the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

Player Control & Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or completely withdraw your advertising consent choices at any time directly within the game. Simply open Settings (⚙️) from the home menu and tap "Consent Choices" to reopen the Google Privacy Options form.

5 Device Permissions

BloomGrid requests only the minimum technical permissions necessary to operate smoothly:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and verify consent status.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows AdMob to check network availability before requesting ad content.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during piece placement and line clears (can be disabled in Settings).
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used exclusively for the optional daily morning reminder. This permission is requested only after completing a game or toggling the reminder, never on initial app launch.

6 Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

BloomGrid is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through our App, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate corrective action.

7 Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Swiss DPA)

Under applicable international data protection regulations, you hold rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You may inquire what data Google AdMob processes in relation to your device identifier.
  • Right to Opt-Out / Restrict Processing: You can opt out of personalized advertising via the in-game Consent Choices menu or your Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will not impact your access to BloomGrid gameplay or core features.

8 Data Retention & Deletion

Because Tech2U Savannah does not maintain user accounts or host backend player databases, we do not retain your personal data on external servers. To erase all locally cached gameplay data, high scores, and settings, you can clear the App's storage via your device settings or uninstall the App.
