1 Overview & 100% Offline Educational Architecture

FX Foundations ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Tech2U Forex (a division of Tech2U Savannah) to provide accessible, beginner-to-advanced retail forex education, market structure analysis, risk management calculators, and interactive quiz evaluations. We believe that learning financial concepts must never come at the expense of user privacy.

Zero Account Registration & Offline-First Curriculum: FX Foundations requires no sign-up, no email address, no phone number, and no user accounts. All 14 course modules, diagrams, educational illustrations, calculator algorithms, and 84 interactive quiz questions are bundled locally in the application package. You can study and complete the full curriculum without an active internet connection.

2 Financial Non-Intermediation & Educational Disclaimer

FX Foundations is strictly an educational tool and market reference app. To ensure complete transparency under Google Play Financial Features policies:

  • No Brokerage or Trading Execution: The App does not offer real-money trading, binary options, CFD execution, or order placement. It does not connect to any broker trading API (such as MetaTrader, cTrader, or REST execution endpoints).
  • No Financial Custody or Deposits: We do not accept, hold, transfer, or process client funds, deposits, withdrawals, cryptocurrencies, or electronic money.
  • No Financial Advice: All materials, examples, quizzes, and calculator tools (Position Sizing, Pip Value, Risk-to-Reward) are provided solely for educational and simulation purposes and do not constitute financial, investment, or legal advice.

3 Information We Do Not Collect

Because the application is designed to operate locally without server accounts, we do not collect, harvest, store, or sell:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as full name, residential address, national ID, or email address.
  • Financial information, bank account credentials, credit/debit card numbers, or transaction histories.
  • Biometric data, contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or device microphone/camera feeds.
  • Precise geolocation coordinates (GPS tracking).

4 Local Data Storage & Preferences

When you use FX Foundations, your learning progress is stored solely on your device using Android's private sandboxed local key-value store (SharedPreferences):

  • Module Progress: Tracks which modules have been completed (e.g., Module 01 through 14).
  • Quiz Scores: Stores your highest score and percentage for each module's 6-question quiz.
  • Display Settings: Stores your appearance mode selection (System Default, Light Mode, or Dark Mode).

This data remains strictly on your local hardware and is never synced to external cloud servers.

5 Device Permissions & Technical Purpose

FX Foundations requests only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used solely to fetch contextual advertising via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and retrieve European User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent preferences. All 14 course modules and trading tools run 100% offline even if these permissions are inactive.

6 Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

To keep this comprehensive 14-module course 100% free for learners, FX Foundations displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). We adhere strictly to international privacy frameworks including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss DPA, and US State Privacy Laws (CCPA/CPRA):

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Before any advertisements are served to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App displays a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialogue. You have full legal authority to grant, customize, or decline advertising consent.

Consent Revocation: You may modify or revoke your consent at any time inside the app by going to Settings → Privacy & Consent → Consent Preferences.

7 Data Retention, User Control & Deletion

You retain complete sovereignty over your local data:

  • Resetting Quiz & Course Progress: You can clear your course completion records and quiz scores at any time via the device's application manager by selecting "Clear Data" or "Clear Storage".
  • Application Removal: Uninstalling FX Foundations immediately purges all locally stored progress, quiz scores, and cached preferences from your device.

8 Children's Privacy

FX Foundations does not target or knowingly solicit information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). The content is developed for young adults and mature learners studying financial literacy and retail currency markets.
