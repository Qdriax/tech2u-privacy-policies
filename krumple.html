1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Krumple: Car Crash Racing (package name: com.tech2u.krumple).

Core Guarantee: Krumple is built strictly on privacy-by-design. We do not require account creation, user logins, cloud registration, or social media linking. All car builds, race results, damage multipliers, coin balances, and settings remain stored solely on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts.

Any telemetry or technical identifiers required for ad serving and consent verification are handled strictly by certified third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform) as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Krumple stores your game progress locally within the sandboxed application environment:

  • Coin balances and unlocked vehicles (6 distinct cars).
  • Vehicle upgrades (Engine, Frame, and Tyres).
  • Visual vehicle paint selections and garage preferences.
  • Championship podium standings, Daily Wreck records, and Last Wheel distance records.
  • Player preferences: Sound effects, Music volume, Haptic vibration, and Daily Reminder toggle.

This data never leaves your device and can be permanently erased at any time by clearing application storage in Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP & AdMob)

Krumple is supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob. We implement Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply fully with global privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In regions where consent or opt-out is required by law (such as the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, and relevant US states), no ad requests or advertising identifiers are processed until you have submitted your preferences via Google's UMP consent form.

Advertisements include:

  • Opt-in Rewarded Ads: Entirely optional ads that double your race payout or patch a blown engine. You choose whether and when to watch them.
  • Interstitials: Infrequent interstitial ads displayed strictly between completed race score screens with strict cooldown timers (never during a race).
  • Adaptive Banners: Non-intrusive banners displayed only on quiet menu screens (Home and Garage).

5 Device Permissions

Krumple requests only the minimum permissions necessary for gameplay and ad operations:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ads and display Google UMP privacy consent dialogues.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by the network stack to check whether an active internet connection is available.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Powers responsive physical haptic feedback when colliding with barriers, crashing panels, and shredding parts (can be toggled off at any time in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used solely if you choose to enable the optional 9:00 AM Daily Wreck reminder notification. You can enable or disable this at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

6 Optional Morning Reminder

If you toggle on the daily reminder in Settings, Krumple schedules a local notification for 9:00 AM local time reminding you of the new Daily Wreck track. This reminder is scheduled purely locally on your device without sending any data to external servers. You can turn it off anytime with one tap in Settings.

7 Children’s Privacy & Family Policy

Krumple is an arcade crash racing game featuring cartoonish vehicle damage with zero human injury, blood, or violence. The game is not targeted towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe any minor has provided data, contact us for immediate remediation.

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under international privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you have full authority over your data:

  • Consent Revocation at Any Time: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game by opening Settings → Privacy options.
  • Data Erasure: All race progression and saved coins live on your device. Clear the app storage in your Android settings to immediately wipe all data.
