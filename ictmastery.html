1 Overview & Offline Educational Architecture

ICT SMC Mastery: Trading Curriculum (referred to as "the Application", "we", "our", or "us", package name com.tech2u.ict_smc_mastery), developed and published by Tech2U Savannah, is designed as an offline-first technical analysis companion.

Offline-First Commitment: All 12 modules, curriculum guides, institutional diagrams, and quizzes are stored locally on your device upon app installation. Reading lessons and taking module quizzes requires zero active internet access and transmits no personal information to our servers.

2 Financial Non-Intermediation & Educational Disclaimer

The Application is strictly an educational tool. It does not provide financial advice, broker services, live trading execution, or trading signals. Trading foreign exchange (Forex), contracts for difference (CFDs), and other financial markets involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for all investors.

Any references to brokers or external regulatory entities are for informational and affiliate transparency purposes only. Users must perform their own independent due diligence before depositing funds with any financial institution.

3 Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. We do not collect, request, or store:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Financial details, trading balances, bank accounts, or payment records.
  • Precise geolocation data (GPS).
  • Personal contacts, biometric identifiers, camera inputs, or device microphones.

4 Local Data Storage & Progress

The Application utilizes on-device key-value storage (SharedPreferences) exclusively to store user learning progress:

  • Completed modules and highest quiz scores.
  • User appearance preferences (System, Light, Dark theme mode).

This information never leaves your device and can be wiped immediately at any time by tapping Reset Progress in the More tab or clearing app data in Android settings.

5 Device Permissions & Technical Purpose

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to request advertising units via Google AdMob and to query regulatory verification links and developer portfolio links when explicitly requested by the user.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables the network layer to gracefully handle offline scenarios without crashing.

6 Advertising & Google UMP Consent Framework

The Application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support ongoing free educational development. In compliance with European GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, and US state privacy regulations (including CCPA/CPRA), we deploy the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

Users in applicable jurisdictions are presented with an interactive consent form upon initial launch to choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or ad partner restrictions. You can modify or withdraw your consent preferences at any time by navigating to More > Consent Preferences.

8 Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not operate remote user accounts, we retain no user data on external servers. All on-device learning history and preferences are automatically deleted if you uninstall the Application.
