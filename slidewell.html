1Introduction & Core Philosophy

This Privacy Policy explains how Slidewell: Tabletop Shuffleboard ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed and distributed by Tech2U Savannah (package: com.tech2u.slidewell), manages user data and privacy.

We believe great tabletop gaming should be transparent and respectful. Slidewell operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not require accounts, logins, passwords, email addresses, or access to sensitive device peripherals.

2Information Handled Locally (Data Minimization)

The app only stores gameplay information strictly on your local device:

  • Match Statistics & Streaks: Matches played, wins, losses, win rate, daily challenges completed, high scores, and streaks.
  • Cosmetic Customization: Unlocked puck skins and active skin selections.
  • Preferences: Audio sound effects, music toggle, haptics, colour-blind mode, reduced motion, graphics quality (high / lite), and local daily notification preferences.

This data is stored solely in local app storage and can be erased instantly at any time via the in-game "Reset progress" option or Android Application Manager.

3Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to fetch non-intrusive advertisements from Google AdMob and verify connectivity status.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely for scheduling the optional daily challenge reminder (9:00 AM local time). Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

Used by Google Play Services to provide compliant advertising identifiers according to user consent selections.

4Advertising & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Slidewell is free to play and supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob. To respect player focus and comply strictly with the Google Play Better Ads Experiences Policy:

  • No Mid-Game Interrupts: We never display full-screen ads during active disc sliding, aiming, collisions, or round gameplay.
  • No Menu Interrupts: Full-screen ads never appear when navigating menus, toggling settings, or selecting skins.
  • Natural Milestones & Cooldown: Occasional full-screen ads appear only after a game round finishes on the Result Screen (every 2nd finished match, never in the first 3 matches, with a strict minimum 3-minute cooldown buffer).
  • Opt-In Rewarded Ads: Rewarded video ads are strictly 100% voluntary (e.g. previewing locked puck skins).
  • Anchored Adaptive Banner: Placed safely at the bottom perimeter of the display on result/stats/skins screens without interfering with gameplay.

Google AdMob & Google Play Services

AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and coarse interaction diagnostics for ad serving and fraud detection.

5Consent Revocation & User Privacy Choices

In compliance with European Union GDPR (Article 7(3)), UK GDPR, and Swiss DPA regulations, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form upon first launch.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can withdraw or adjust your consent preferences at any time. Simply open the in-game Settings gear icon and tap the "Privacy Preferences" button to reopen your consent choices.

6Children's Privacy Compliance

Slidewell is designed as a family-friendly casual tabletop game for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly ensure appropriate remediation.

7Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your region, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what data is processed. Slidewell processes zero personal data on external servers.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all local statistics and records via the in-game reset button or Android application management.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell player personal information for monetary or other considerations.
