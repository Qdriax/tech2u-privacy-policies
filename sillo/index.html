1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile arcade game SILLO: Shadow Puppet Timing (package name: com.tech2u.sillo).

Core Guarantee: SILLO is built with privacy-by-design. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or social profiles. All gameplay progress, high scores, unlocked paper playbills, streak records, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your local device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, or contacts list.

Any technical data processed is handled exclusively through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to facilitate compliant advertising and consent choices, as detailed in Section 4.

3 On-Device Storage

SILLO stores your game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • High scores across Showtime, Daily Matinee, and Quick Draw modes.
  • Scoreboard statistics: total shows played, puppets matched, perfect shadows, full houses, great-or-better accuracy rate, best streak, furthest act, and total time at the lamp.
  • Unlocked paper playbills: Kraft Matinee, Newsprint, Origami, Felt Workshop, Lantern Night, Blueprint, Tin Toys, and Gala Gold.
  • Player settings: sound effects, tactile haptics, colour-blind mode, reduced motion, low quality render governor, and morning reminder preferences.

This data never leaves your device and can be wiped anytime by clearing the app data in Android Settings or via the in-game Reset button on the Scoreboard.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

SILLO is supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~4849208748). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ensure full compliance with global privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent Management: For users in regulated jurisdictions (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no ad requests or personalized identifiers are initialized until consent preferences have been established via the interactive Google UMP dialog.

Ad formats and placement rules in SILLO comply strictly with Google Better Ads standards:

  • Rewarded Ads: Completely optional player choice to "Relight a wick" in Showtime or "+15 seconds" in Quick Draw (limited to once per show), or to double your final score via the "Encore bonus" on the result screen. Daily Matinee and Duet modes feature zero rewarded ads to keep competition fair.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped transitions shown only when exiting the score screen (never during the first 2 shows of a session, at most every 2nd show, at least 90 seconds apart, and never after achieving a new high score).
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively on static screens (Home, menus, result screen). Ads never appear while puppets are on stage.
  • App Open Ads: Capped strictly to returning to an idle Home screen after 2+ minutes away, at most once per 4 hours; never on cold start and never on the first two launches.

5 Device Permissions

SILLO requests only essential permissions necessary to operate:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to retrieve ad content and Google UMP consent forms.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the app to check whether an active network connection is available before requesting ad inventory.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides crisp haptic feedback when shadows fit and frames snap (can be toggled in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used solely if you explicitly enable the optional 09:00 AM morning reminder when the new Daily Matinee acts are ready. Asked once after your first finished show and can be turned on/off anytime in Settings.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family-Friendly Design

SILLO is a stylized timing game designed for general audiences (ages 13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt corrective action.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under global data privacy laws, you have full authority over your data:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game by tapping Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Data Deletion: Since all player statistics are stored on-device, deleting the application or clearing application cache in Android Settings permanently removes all records.
