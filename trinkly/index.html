1 Overview & Core Philosophy

Trinkly: 3D Triple Match ("Trinkly", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a relaxing 3D triple-match puzzle game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.trinkly).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Trinkly requires NO player accounts, NO logins, NO phone numbers, and NO cloud synchronization backends. All game progression, stars, coin balances, tool counts, unlocked trinkets, and settings remain stored strictly on your device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, contacts, or phone number.

Any technical data processing is strictly performed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as detailed below.

3 On-Device Storage & Data Minimization

In accordance with data minimization principles, Trinkly stores gameplay data locally in sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Level progress (Market path levels 1 to 28+), stars earned per level, and failure counts (for pile-lightening mercy mechanics).
  • Coin balances and earned utility tool inventory (Shake, Put-back, Grabber, Spare slot).
  • Mode records: Daily Crate streak & calendar records, Rummage Rush best scores, and Zen Bin statistics.
  • Career statistics: total wins, first-try clears, total attempts, triples popped, shakes used, and Zen sessions.
  • Preferences: sound effects toggle, background music toggle, haptic vibration, screen shake (reduced motion), and optional 09:00 morning reminder.
  • Offline backup string: an exportable alphanumeric text code allowing manual transfer of saves to another device without an account.

This data never leaves your device and can be permanently cleared at any time by clearing application storage in Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Trinkly is supported by mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~8524372053). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Trinkly strictly adhere to Google Play Better Ads Standards and NEVER interrupt active puzzle gameplay:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on calm menu screens and result cards; automatically hidden during active crate rummaging.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only after a result card behind an announced "Ad break"; never during the first 5 wins, at most once every 3 wins, and minimum 3 minutes apart.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary player choices to refill a handy tool or double coins on the win card. Choosing a rewarded ad resets the interstitial frequency timer.
  • No App-Open Ads: To prevent intrusive prestitials, Trinkly contains zero app-open ads.

5 Device Permissions

Trinkly requests only essential technical Android permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to query UMP consent and load compliant ads when connected to the internet.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows the ad engine to detect network connectivity and prevent unnecessary network calls when offline.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback when picking, sliding, and popping matching trinkets.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly if you enable the optional 09:00 Daily Crate reminder in Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows the local notification manager to restore your chosen 09:00 alarm schedule after device reboots.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Trinkly contains no adult content, no violence, and no social chat mechanisms. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You maintain full control over your privacy rights at all times:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can open and reconfigure your advertising privacy choices at any moment directly from the in-game Settings menu via the Privacy Options button, which launches the Google UMP consent form.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game data is stored locally, clearing application storage in Android Settings instantly deletes all records and saves permanently.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You may also opt out of interest-based ads via Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
