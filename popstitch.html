1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Popstitch: Button Blast Puzzle (package name: com.tech2u.popstitch).

Core Guarantee: Popstitch is designed with privacy-first engineering. We require no user accounts, no passwords, no email registration, and no cloud logins. All quilt progress, level records, earned coins, unlocked fabrics, and settings remain strictly on your local device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number.

Data transmission from the application is strictly limited to Google's certified Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for serving policy-compliant advertisements and managing privacy consent choices in supported jurisdictions.

3 On-Device Storage & Progress Backup

Popstitch saves your puzzle state entirely on your device using sandboxed local storage:

  • Quilt patchwork progress, completed levels (500+ handcrafted boards), and high scores.
  • Earned coins, unlocked sewing tools, and fabric swatches.
  • Gameplay statistics (first-try win rate, biggest pop chain, longest Hot Run streak).
  • Game settings (sound effects, synthesizer music, haptic vibrations, and daily reminder preference).
  • Anonymous offline backup code (PS1 format) generated purely on-device to allow manual transfer of your progress to a new device without an account.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing application data in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Popstitch is supported by advertising delivered via Google AdMob. We implement Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with international data privacy regulations, including:

  • European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR)
  • United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
  • Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) and applicable US state privacy laws

Fair Ad Experience: Advertisements in Popstitch strictly adhere to Google Play Better Ads policies. Ads never interrupt an active puzzle board or level in progress. Rewarded ads (such as +3 extra mercy moves or doubling level completion coins) are 100% voluntary and player-initiated. Interstitials only appear between levels behind a dedicated ad-break notice with cooldown protections.

5 Device Permissions

Popstitch requests only the minimal technical permissions required for gameplay, ad compliance, and optional local alerts:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required to load ad inventory and fetch privacy consent forms via Google UMP.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to verify active internet connectivity.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Powers tactile haptic feedback when popping buttons, detonating scissors/pincushions/spools, and clearing goals (can be toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used strictly if you explicitly enable the optional 09:00 AM Daily Swatch reminder. Can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Ensures scheduled local daily puzzle reminders persist across device reboots when enabled.

6 Children’s Privacy

Popstitch is built for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt remedial action.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

In full compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, you have complete sovereignty over your privacy choices:

  • On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game: tap Settings → Privacy / Consent Choices to open the Google UMP consent form.
  • Data Deletion: Because all game records are stored locally on your device, deleting app storage via Android settings erases all your data instantly.
