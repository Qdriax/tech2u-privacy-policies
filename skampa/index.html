1 Overview & Core Principles

Skampa: Market Monkey Run ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a 3D endless runner game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.skampa) following Kito the monkey sprinting through a sunny golden-hour fruit market.

Offline-First & Privacy-First: Skampa requires NO accounts, NO cloud sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO external player tracking. All game saves, high scores, unlocked characters, upgrades, and mango balances remain strictly stored on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, phone number, or contacts.

Any technical data processing is strictly handled by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to deliver compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as described below.

3 On-Device Storage & Zero Data Loss

Skampa saves your game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Double Checksummed Saves: Two alternating checksummed save slots written after every run and every 5 seconds during active runs to eliminate file corruption.
  • Mid-Run Crash Snapshot: If your phone battery dies or the app closes mid-run, Skampa offers a free, seamless "Resume Run" at the exact meter with no penalties and no forced ads.
  • Accountless Save Transfer: A secure local transfer code moves your save to a new phone without needing an online account or email registration.
  • Runners & Power-ups: Character unlocks (Kito, Pembe, Zuri, Bongo) and level-up tiers for Mango Magnet, Market Kite, Spring Sandals, and Coconut Helmet.
  • Game Records: Daily Dash best score, 3 daily goal completions, banked mangoes, and longest distance.
  • Audio & Notification Settings: Granular toggles for Music, Sound Effects, Haptic Vibration, and optional morning reminder.

This data stays 100% on your device and can be erased anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Skampa > Storage > Clear Data.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

Skampa is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~9863384789). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in Skampa strictly adhere to Google Better Ads Standards and never interrupt active gameplay:

  • No Ads Mid-Run: No ads ever appear while you are actively running or dodging obstacles.
  • No App-Open Ads: There is never an ad on app launch or startup.
  • Rewarded Ads (100% Opt-In): "Second wind" to revive once per run after a crash, and "Double mangoes" on the results screen. Rewards are credited immediately upon completion.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped between runs only. Never shown before your 3rd run, limited to at most 1 per 3 runs and 3 minutes, and never within 90 seconds of a rewarded ad. Every interstitial is followed by a "Ready?" tap sheet so the game never abruptly launches into action.
  • Adaptive Banners: Shown exclusively on non-gameplay screens (Home, Runners, and Results). Banners are never displayed during runs.

5 Device Permissions

Skampa requests only essential technical permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements and verifying UMP consent.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to verify network connectivity for ad serving and offline mode.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic feedback during jumps, slides, crashes, and obstacle dodges (can be disabled in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly for an optional daily 9:00 AM market reminder (prompted on Android 13+, easily toggled off in Settings).

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows rescheduling the daily morning reminder if the device restarts, only if enabled by the player.

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

Skampa is an arcade running game designed for general audiences and players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is requested or collected, Skampa offers a safe, family-friendly gaming experience.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time via the in-game Settings. Google UMP enables on-demand privacy preference adjustments whenever you choose.

To delete all locally stored game data, go to Android Settings > Apps > Skampa > Storage > Clear Data.
