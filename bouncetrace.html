1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

BounceTrace: Ricochet Puzzle ("the App") is developed and maintained by Tech2U Savannah ("we", "us", or "our"). The App is an offline, non-violent, solver-certified bank-shot puzzle game where player solutions are captured as collectible vector line art.

Privacy by Architecture: BounceTrace was engineered with a strict offline-first doctrine. No accounts, no internet connectivity, no analytics beacons, and no background profiling. Nothing leaves your device.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal information from users of BounceTrace. Specifically, the App does NOT access or gather:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Precise or approximate device geolocation data.
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address) or network parameters.
  • Biometric data, contacts, calendar entries, SMS messages, or call logs.
  • Behavioral telemetry or telemetry tracking across other applications.

3 Local Data Storage & Tracebook

All gameplay data generated during your play sessions is stored strictly on your local device using Android's encrypted private application sandbox (Room / SQLite database and SharedPreferences):

  • Tracebook Records: When you clear a puzzle board, the polyline vector route and milestone metrics (board ID, par, arrows used, timestamp) are stored locally in your private Tracebook so you can inspect and replay your solutions.
  • Settings & Preferences: Your selected visual and haptic preferences (Aim Assist, Left-Handed alignment, High Contrast, Reduced Motion, Sound, and Haptic feedback) are stored locally.

This data is sandboxed and cannot be accessed by other applications. If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, all locally stored game data is permanently removed.

4 Device Permissions

BounceTrace adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization and least privilege. The App declares zero sensitive or dangerous device permissions in its manifest:

  • No Internet Permission: The App does not declare android.permission.INTERNET. It cannot establish outgoing or incoming network connections.
  • No Storage Permission: The App does not declare shared external storage read/write permissions.
  • No Location, Camera, or Audio Permissions: The App has no access to cameras, microphones, or sensor locations.

5 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

BounceTrace contains no advertisements and incorporates no advertising or tracking software development kits (SDKs). There are no banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, rewarded video ads, or programmatic trackers.

6 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because BounceTrace collects no personal data whatsoever and does not require internet communication, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation child-privacy standards (GDPR-K). The App is safe for audiences of all ages, including children under 13.

7 Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Swiss DPA)

We honor global privacy rights including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: Since we collect no personal data, there is no remote data profile to retrieve.
  • Right to Deletion: All data resides on your physical phone; uninstalling the App or clearing App data via Android Settings instantly deletes all local records.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Data: We do not sell, rent, or share personal data under any circumstances.

8 Data Retention & Deletion

We do not operate servers that host player accounts or telemetry databases. Your puzzle progress and trace artifacts remain on your handset for as long as you keep the application installed. You retain total ownership and control over your data at all times.
