1 Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to KLONK: Shape Drop Timing Game ("the App"), developed by Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us"). We believe in absolute user privacy, zero registration friction, and complete transparency. KLONK is engineered as a standalone, offline-first 3D timing arcade game with no account requirements, no server synchronization, and zero personal identity tracking.

Core Guarantee: We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, address book, photos, or files. You never need an account or sign-in to enjoy the full game.

2 Information Handled Strictly On-Device

All gameplay states and preferences are processed and stored strictly on your local device within the application's isolated sandboxed storage (localStorage under key klonk and local SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Game Records & Statistics: High scores in Classic, Daily Drum, Blitz 60, and Duel modes; highest streak multipliers; total klonks; perfect drops; and game session count.
  • Unlocked Customization: Unlocked toy themes (Nursery Wood, Gumdrop, Chalkboard, Terrazzo, Seaside, Bedtime, Brass Band).
  • User Preferences: Haptic vibration toggle, sound effects switch, reduced motion preference, and high-contrast colour-blind accessibility mode.
  • Daily Drum Challenge Seed: The local UTC calendar date hash used to generate the deterministic daily puzzle.

This data remains on your physical hardware at all times. It is never transmitted to external cloud servers and can be wiped instantly by clearing app data in Android Settings.

3 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance and provide KLONK as a 100% free-to-play game, we partner with Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements. Ad formats are carefully restricted per Google Better Ads standards and policy guidelines:

  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively on non-gameplay screens (Home, Scoreboard, Toybox). Banners are strictly suppressed during active drum gameplay, intermission, and pause states.
  • Interstitial Ads: Frequency-capped full-screen ads shown exclusively between completed runs (never on first two sessions, never after setting a new high score, and spaced at least 2 minutes apart).
  • Rewarded Ads: Fully voluntary video ads triggered only by explicit player tap (e.g., to continue an ended run once per session, or to immediately unlock a toy set in the Toybox).
  • App Open Ads: Lightly configured only upon returning to an idle Home screen after 4+ hours of inactivity, never interrupting active sessions or cold starts.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address (for coarse regional ad localization and fraud prevention), and app crash telemetry. For complete details, review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4 Consent Management & Revocation (Google UMP)

We enforce strict compliance with European (GDPR), United Kingdom (UK GDPR), Swiss (Swiss DPA), and US state privacy regulations (California CCPA/CPRA). We integrate the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Consent is queried and collected before the Mobile Ads SDK is initialized or any ad requests are dispatched.
  • In regions requiring opt-in consent (e.g., the EEA and UK), non-personalized ads or consent requests adhere strictly to user choices.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent choices at any time directly within the game. Simply open Settings → Privacy Choices to invoke the official Google consent options dialogue.

5 Device Permissions & Local Notifications

KLONK requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables ad retrieval and UMP consent verification.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Prevents unnecessary network queries when offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Drives tactile haptic sensations on shape snaps, strikes, and button taps.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & Alarm Permissions: Used exclusively to schedule the optional morning reminder (at 09:00 local time) alerting you that a new Daily Drum puzzle is available. All reminders are scheduled entirely on-device using local Android AlarmManager APIs—no remote notification servers or tokens are used. You may enable or disable this reminder at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

We do not request access to sensitive device hardware or personal data (no camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, phone status, or external storage).

6 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

KLONK is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older (16 and older in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13. Ad inventory is configured to exclude inappropriate or mature categories.

7 Global Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Swiss DPA)

Users in the European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and California enjoy specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Know / Access / Delete: Because we do not transmit or store identifiable personal data on external servers, the most direct way to exercise your data rights is by clearing local application data on your Android device.
  • Opt-Out of Data Sale / Targeted Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time using the in-app "Privacy Choices" menu button or by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your Android system Google Settings. We do not sell user personal information.

8 Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because all gameplay data is kept locally on your phone, you retain 100% control over retention. To erase all records, scores, unlocked toy sets, and settings, navigate to your device's Settings → Apps → KLONK → Storage & Cache and tap Clear Storage (or uninstall the application).
