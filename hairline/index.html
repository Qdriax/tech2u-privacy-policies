1 Overview & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech2U Savannah ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Hairline: Traffic Racer 3D (package name: com.tech2u.hairline).

Core Guarantee: Hairline is built around privacy-by-design. We do not require user accounts, logins, cloud sync, or social network linkage. All high scores, closest shave records, car unlocks, and settings are saved locally on your device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, phone number, or biometric data.

Any telemetry or advertising identifier transmission is strictly handled by certified third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform) as described below.

3 On-Device Storage

Hairline saves game state locally using sandboxed browser/device storage (key: hairline.v1):

  • Personal high scores, best distance, and closest centimetre near-miss passes.
  • Unlocked vehicles (Hatchback, Coupe, Muscle, Wedge, Tuk-Tuk, Prototype) and paint customisations.
  • Coin balances earned through gameplay and daily missions.
  • Settings (sound volume, synthwave music, haptics, and daily reminder toggle).

This data remains strictly on your device and can be erased at any time by clearing application storage in Android system settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP & AdMob)

Hairline is supported by mobile advertising delivered through Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~7580391867). Ad formats include rewarded video ads (optional Keep Driving and double coins), non-intrusive interstitials between completed runs, and home/garage banners.

To comply with global data protection laws (EU GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, and US State privacy laws including CCPA/CPRA), Hairline integrates Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

Consent First: In regions requiring consent, no ad requests are initialized and no advertising identifiers (AAID) are accessed until user consent is requested and obtained.

5 Device Permissions

Hairline requests only essential system permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Used to fetch ad inventory and load UMP consent forms via Google AdMob.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used by network and ad libraries to verify active connectivity.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used for tactile feedback during near-miss passes, scrapes, and collisions. Can be toggled off at any time in Settings.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used solely if you enable the optional 09:00 AM Daily Road notification. Can be toggled off anytime in Settings.

6 Optional Morning Notification

The app features an optional daily local notification at 09:00 AM local time reminding you of the new Daily Road challenge. This notification is generated locally on your device without sending any data to external servers. You can turn this notification on or off at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

7 Children’s Privacy

Hairline is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

You retain full rights under applicable data protection laws:

  • Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or revoke your consent preferences at any time directly in the game by tapping Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Data Deletion: You can wipe all locally stored scores, unlocked cars, and preferences by clearing app data in your Android settings.
