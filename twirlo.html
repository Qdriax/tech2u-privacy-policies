1 Overview & Core Principles

TWIRLO ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tech2U Savannah, is a tactile 3D portrait color-match arcade game for Android (Package ID: com.tech2u.twirlo).

Offline-First & Privacy-First: TWIRLO requires NO accounts, NO sign-ins, NO personal profiles, and NO cloud registration. All gameplay progress, high scores, collected bead sets, and user preferences remain stored strictly on your personal device.

2 Data We Collect

We do not collect, harvest, sell, share, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, contacts, or phone number.

Any technical data processing is strictly performed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display compliant advertisements and manage consent preferences as outlined below.

3 On-Device Storage

TWIRLO saves your game state locally using sandboxed device storage (browser localStorage within the embedded WebView and Android SharedPreferences):

  • High scores across Spin Shift, Daily Spindle, and Rush 90 game modes.
  • Scoreboard statistics: total popped beads, longest chain cascades, and career games played.
  • Unlocked bead sets (Gumdrops, Buttons, Pearls, Pebbles, Lanterns, Gems, and Toy Blocks).
  • Accessibility and user preferences: sound effects, haptic vibrations, colour-blind mode with geometric markers, reduced motion, low quality graphics setting, and optional morning reminder preference.

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings.

4 Advertising & Consent (Google UMP)

TWIRLO is supported by player-respectful mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-6234189010245060, App ID: ca-app-pub-6234189010245060~5140050455). We integrate Google’s certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to strictly comply with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), United Kingdom GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Consent First: In applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states), no personalized ads or advertising identifiers are requested until you have submitted your consent choices via the interactive Google UMP consent form.

Ad placements in TWIRLO strictly follow Google's Better Ads Standards and never interrupt active ring twists or cascades:

  • Rewarded Video: Completely optional player choices: (a) "Continue" to clear lower tower beads after topping out once per run, or (b) "Slow Feeder" to slow bead drops for 60 seconds in Spin Shift.
  • Interstitial Ads: Capped transitions shown only when leaving a game over/result screen (never on new personal bests, frequency capped, and with cooldown periods).
  • App Open Ads: Shown only on returning to an idle Home screen after 4+ hours away, never on cold starts or during active gameplay.
  • Adaptive Banners: Displayed exclusively in an isolated strip below quiet menu screens (Home, Scores, and Bead Box). Never shown during active twisting play.

5 Device Permissions

TWIRLO requests only minimal technical permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET

Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements and verifying UMP consent.

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Allows checking connection state for offline gameplay and ad loading.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Provides tactile haptic clicks when twisting rings and popping beads (can be turned off in Settings).

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used strictly for an optional daily 9:00 AM puzzle reminder (disabled by default or opt-in, easily toggled off in Settings).

6 Children’s Privacy & Family Friendly Design

TWIRLO is designed for general audiences of all ages who enjoy spatial puzzles. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is requested or collected, TWIRLO provides a safe, non-intrusive environment for all players.

7 Your Rights & Consent Revocation

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

On-Demand Consent Revocation: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time by opening the in-game Settings screen and tapping Privacy Settings. This immediately relaunches Google's official privacy preferences form.

To delete all locally stored game data, go to Android Settings > Apps > TWIRLO > Storage > Clear Data.
